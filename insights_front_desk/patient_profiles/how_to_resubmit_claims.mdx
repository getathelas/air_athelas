### At a Glance

Insights offers several ways to modify and resubmit claims. This guide covers resubmitting one or more of a single patient's claims from their profile.

<Note>
  Resubmitting claims from the patient profile may not be enabled for every site. If you don't see the **Resubmit** action, use the biller [resubmit a single claim](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim) or [resubmit claims in bulk](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_claims_in_bulk) flows instead.
</Note>

### Here's How to Do It

[Open the patient's profile](/insights_front_desk/patient_profiles/how_to_find_and_edit_a_patients_profile) and go to the **Claims** tab.

Check the boxes for the claims you want to resubmit, then click **Resubmit**.

In the popup, you can change the **Insurance Priority** (primary, secondary, tertiary) for Professional, Workers' Comp, Auto, or Institutional claims, and update the insurance information. You can also **Create New Insurance** here and select it from the dropdown.

<Note>
  When you update insurance information in Insights, update it in your EHR too. Insights imports from your EHR nightly, so you can also update the EHR and wait a day for the new information to appear — reducing the chance of a manual error.
</Note>

Click **Confirm Resubmission**. A confirmation popup notes that the selected encounters will be marked as **Resubmitted**.

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Can I resubmit claims for more than one patient at once here?">
  This flow resubmits claims for the single patient whose profile you're on. To work across patients, use the biller [bulk resubmit](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_claims_in_bulk) flow.
</Accordion>
