#### Adding Insurance

Add insurance while creating a patient or from the **Demographics** section of an existing patient's profile. Select the **Insurance Company**, which sets the **Plan Type**, then enter:

- **Policy Number** and **Group Number**
- **Effective Date** and **Expiration Date**
- **Guarantor** — the relationship to the policyholder (defaults to **Self**). If the relationship is not Self, open the guarantor fields and enter the policyholder's first name, last name, email, phone, date of birth, gender, and home address.
- **Insurance Card** — optionally upload a photo of the card
- **Require prior auth on submission** — toggle if this insurance requires a prior authorization

Click **Create** (or **Save**) when done.

#### Special Insurance Types

- **Workers' Compensation** — selecting a workers' comp insurance company automatically sets the plan type to Workers' Compensation (you can also set it manually). Extra fields appear, including **Claim Number**, **Accident Date**, and — depending on your site's settings — **Employer** details. For auto/accident claims, an **Accident State** is required.
- **Self-Pay** — type **Self Pay** in the Insurance Company field and select it; the insurance detail fields are replaced with **Self-Pay (No Insurance)**.
- **Missing Insurance** — if you don't have the patient's insurance yet, type **Missing Insurance** in the Insurance Company field and select it.

#### Editing Insurance

To edit insurance later, open the patient's profile, go to the **Demographics** section, and edit the insurance entry.

Patients also have the ability to update their own Insurance and Demographics information via the Patient Portal. When these changes are submitted, you will be notified of the request in the 

- After submitting, patients see **Request sent for approval** instead of **Information updated successfully**.
- The changed field shows a **Pending review** indicator. The previously confirmed value stays displayed until staff act on the request.
- If staff approve, the new value is displayed. If staff reject, the indicator clears and the original value remains.

After a patient submits updated insurance, update their insurance in your EHR and mark the task **Completed** here so the list stays accurate. If your site uses the Front Desk Inbox, the submission arrives as a review request. Approve it, or modify and then approve it, in the Front Desk Inbox instead of re-entering it.

If your site uses the Front Desk Inbox, demographic and insurance changes submitted by the patient (from the Patient Portal, kiosk, Patient Hub, or the Patient Update and Insurance Update forms) are not applied here right away. A **pending review** indicator appears on the patient's Demographics and Insurance pages until the request is approved or rejected. Click the indicator to open the request in the Front Desk Inbox.

When the Front Desk Inbox is enabled, insurance added or updated by patients is held for front desk approval before it is written to the chart.

hen the Front Desk Inbox is enabled for your site, insurance and personal information changes submitted in the portal are sent to your front desk for review instead of updating the patient's chart immediately.

- After submitting, patients see **Request sent for approval** instead of **Information updated successfully**.
- The changed field shows a **Pending review** indicator. The previously confirmed value stays displayed until staff act on the request.
- If staff approve, the new value is displayed. If staff reject, the indicator clears and the original value remains.

<Warning>
  Patients are not notified in the portal when a request is rejected. If a rejection needs follow-up, contact the patient directly, for example through [Messages](/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages).
</Warning>

### FAQ

<Accordion title="What if the policyholder isn't the patient?">
  Set the **Guarantor** relationship to something other than **Self**, then fill in the policyholder's name, contact details, date of birth, gender, and address.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I record a self-pay patient?">
  Type **Self Pay** into the Insurance Company field and select it. The insurance details collapse to **Self-Pay (No Insurance)**.
</Accordion>
