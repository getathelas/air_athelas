Sometimes a patient wants to pay with multiple payment methods — for example, two credit cards, or a partial cash payment with the rest on a card. The easiest way to handle this is to **add the entire amount to the patient's balance**, then take **two partial payments** towards that balance.

## Step 1: Add the full amount to the balance

In the **Charge** window, enter the total amount to be collected. Under **Payment Method**, select **Add to Patient's Balance**, then click **Confirm**.

![Charge window with Add to Patient's Balance selected](/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment_1.png)

## Step 2: Collect the first payment

Click on the patient's name from the **Appointments** page to access their patient profile.

![Opening the patient profile from the Appointments page](/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment_2.png)

In the patient profile, select the same charge and click **Pay Balance**. Enter the amount for the first payment and choose the payment method for that amount.

![Selecting the charge and clicking Pay Balance](/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment_3.png)

![Entering the first partial payment amount and method](/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment_4.png)

## Step 3: Collect the second payment

You'll see that the balance on that charge has decreased by the amount you just collected. For the second payment, select the same charge and click **Pay Balance** again.

![Charge balance decreased after first payment](/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment_5.png)

Enter the amount for the second payment and choose the payment method.

![Entering the second partial payment amount and method](/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment/how_to_take_a_partial_or_split_payment_6.png)

<Tip>
  ✨**Smart Tip:** The same flow works for **more than two** payment methods — keep clicking **Pay Balance** on the same charge and collecting partial amounts until the balance reaches `$0`.
</Tip>

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Can I split a payment across more than two methods?">
  Yes. Repeat **Step 3** as many times as needed. Each click of **Pay Balance** on the same charge collects another partial payment against the remaining balance. Continue until the charge balance reaches `$0`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What payment methods can I use for each part of a split payment?">
  Any payment method available in Insights — credit/debit card, saved card on file, cash, check, or an Athelas card reader transaction. Each partial payment can use a different method.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if I add the wrong total to the patient's balance in Step 1?">
  Edit the original charge or void it before collecting payments. Once payments have started posting against the balance, you may need to refund a partial payment first. See [How to refund a payment](/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/how_to_refund_a_payment).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How is a split payment recorded in the patient's PR timeline?">
  Each partial payment appears as a separate event on the patient's [PR timeline](/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/pr_timeline) with its own amount, payment method, and timestamp — making it easy to reconcile both transactions later.
</Accordion>
