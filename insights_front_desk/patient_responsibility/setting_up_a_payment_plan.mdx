#### Here’s How to Do It

**Before you start:** Open **Patient Responsibility** and confirm which patient needs a payment plan.

From the [Patient Responsibility](https://insights.athelas.com/v2/patient-responsibility) page, click on the Actions menu and choose Set Up Payment Plan.

<img src="/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/setting_up_a_payment_plan/setting_up_a_payment_plan_1.png" alt="Patient Responsibility Actions menu with the payment plan option" />

In the popup, search for the patient in question.

<img src="/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/setting_up_a_payment_plan/setting_up_a_payment_plan_2.png" alt="Patient search in the payment plan form" />

Fill in start date and frequency of payments, max amount per charge, and choose a credit card or add a new card.

For a monthly plan, you can select the 29th, 30th, or 31st as the start date. In a shorter month, the payment runs on the last day of that month. It returns to your selected day when the month is long enough. The **Start date** info icon explains this behavior in both the create and update forms.

You’ll also have the opportunity to use outstanding balance and/or to set a fixed amount per payment.

<img src="/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/setting_up_a_payment_plan/setting_up_a_payment_plan_3.png" alt="Payment plan form with schedule, amount, and payment method fields" />

Click **Set Up Payment Plan**, and you’re done! You can now see a Payment Plan indicator on the patient’s listing, under their Current Balance.

<img src="/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/setting_up_a_payment_plan/setting_up_a_payment_plan_4.png" alt="Payment Plan indicator under the patient's current balance" />

### FAQ

<Accordion title="What happens when a monthly payment is scheduled for the 31st?">
In a month without a 31st, the payment runs on that month’s last day. It returns to the 31st in the next month that has one.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I review the monthly date behavior while editing a plan?">
Select the info icon next to **Start date** in the create or update payment plan form.
</Accordion>
