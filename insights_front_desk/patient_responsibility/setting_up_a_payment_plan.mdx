#### Here’s How to Do It

**Prerequisites:** Confirm the patient and the card you want to use for the plan. Your role must also allow you to manage payment plans. If you do not see **Set Up Payment Plan**, ask an administrator to update your role.

From the [Patient Responsibility](https://insights.athelas.com/v2/patient-responsibility) page, click on the Actions menu and choose Set Up Payment Plan.

<img src="/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/setting_up_a_payment_plan/setting_up_a_payment_plan_1.webp" alt="The Patient Responsibility page with the Actions menu open and Set Up Payment Plan highlighted" style={{ width:"100%" }} />

In the popup, search for the patient in question.

<img src="/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/setting_up_a_payment_plan/setting_up_a_payment_plan_2.webp" alt="Searching for a patient by name in the Set Up Payment Plan dialog" style={{ width:"70%" }} />

Fill in **Mode** — which sets whether the plan draws down the patient's outstanding balance or charges a fixed amount per payment — then the frequency of payments, the start date, and the max amount per charge, and choose a credit card or add a new card.

For a monthly plan, you can choose the 29th, 30th, or 31st as the start date. In a shorter month, the payment falls on that month's final day and returns to your selected day in the next month that includes it.

You’ll also have the opportunity to use outstanding balance and/or to set a fixed amount per payment.

<img src="/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/setting_up_a_payment_plan/setting_up_a_payment_plan_3.webp" alt="The Set Up Payment Plan dialog showing the Mode, Frequency, Start date, Max amount per charge, and Preferred credit card fields above the Create Payment Plan button" style={{ width:"70%" }} />

Click `Create Payment Plan`, and you’re done! You can now see a Payment Plan indicator on the patient’s listing, under their Current Balance.

<img src="/images/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/setting_up_a_payment_plan/setting_up_a_payment_plan_4.png" alt="Payment Plan indicator on a patient balance" />

### FAQ

<Accordion title="What happens when my monthly payment date does not exist in a shorter month?">
  The payment falls on the final day of that month. The plan returns to your selected day in the next month that includes it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use a fixed amount for each payment?">
  Yes. During setup, you can use the patient's outstanding balance or set a fixed amount per payment.
</Accordion>
