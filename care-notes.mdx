Care Notes let care teams document clinically relevant, non-visit patient interactions, including phone calls, caregiver messages, physician-to-physician communication, faxes, and other correspondence, directly on the patient's timeline alongside visit notes.

## Care Notes General Guide

To access Care Notes, navigate to a patient's **Visits & Notes** tab.

![Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 46 46 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-13-at-2.46.46-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 46 46 PM")

Care note rows appear merged into the same timeline as visit rows, distinguished by the **Clinical Note Type** column showing **"Care Note"**.

![Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 49 46 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-13-at-2.49.46-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 49 46 PM")

### Care Note statuses

Each care note has one of three statuses:

- **Open** (blue): editable, can receive new entries
- **Closed** (grey): read-only, exportable
- **Archived**: hidden from the default view

![Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 51 21 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-13-at-2.51.21-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 51 21 PM")

### Creating a Care Note

Click the **Care Note** button in the page header to open the creation drawer.

![Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 53 00 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-13-at-2.53.00-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 53 00 PM")

The creation form includes:

- **Note** (required): the free-text entry, written in a rich-text composer
- **Case** (optional)
- **Source** (optional): who provided the information for this note (Patient, PCP / Physician, Hospital, Fax, etc.)
- **Attachment** (optional): one existing patient attachment
- **Assign member as task** (optional): creates a task for a teammate, linked back to this note

![Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 54 08 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-13-at-2.54.08-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 54 08 PM")

Click **Create Care Note** to save. The drawer switches into edit/view mode, and the note appears as a new **Open** row in the table.

![Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 54 50 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-13-at-2.54.50-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 2 54 50 PM")

### The Activity thread

Once a note is created, all updates render as an append-only, immutable thread in the **Activity** section of the drawer. New note entries, metadata changes, assignments, closures, exports, and faxes are all logged with the actor and timestamp.

![Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 3 00 10 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-13-at-3.00.10-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 13 At 3 00 10 PM")

### Filtering the timeline

Use the **Filters** drawer to toggle **Show Visits** / **Show Care Notes**, filter by date range or case, and toggle **Show Archived** to reveal archived notes.

## Example Workflows

### Document a non-visit patient interaction

**Steps:**

1. From the patient's Visits & Notes page, click **Care Note**.
2. Enter the note body describing the interaction (e.g. a phone call summary).
3. Select a **Source** describing who provided the information.
4. Optionally select a **Case** if the note is related to an active case.
5. Click **Create Care Note**.

Screen Recording 2026-08-13 at 3.03.06 [PM.mov](http://PM.mov)

### Add supporting documentation to a Care Note

**Steps:**

1. Open an existing Care Note, or start creating a new one.
2. In the **Attachment** field, select an existing patient attachment.
3. Save (**Create Care Note** or **Update Care Note**).
4. Once the note is closed, export it as a PDF. The attachment is appended after the note content.

Screen Recording 2026-08-13 at 3.05.07 [PM.mov](http://PM.mov)

### Hand off follow-up as a task

**Steps:**

1. Open a Care Note.
2. In **Assign member as task**, select a teammate.
3. Click **Update Care Note** (or **Create Care Note**, if new).
4. Navigate to the **Tasks** page to find the generated task, titled "Care journey note: \{patient name\}".
5. From the task, click **Open care note** to return to the note.

Screen Recording 2026-08-13 at 3.08.05 [PM.mov](http://PM.mov)

### Continue and reassign a Care Note

**Steps:**

1. Open an existing Open care note.
2. Add a new entry in the composer.
3. Change the assignee to a different teammate.
4. Click **Update Care Note**.
5. Review the Activity feed for the "added a new entry" and "reassigned from X to Y" events.

Screen Recording 2026-08-13 at 3.06.25 [PM.mov](http://PM.mov)

### Close a resolved Care Note

**Steps:**

1. Open the note.
2. Click **Close note and Associated Tasks** in the drawer footer.
3. Confirm with **Close Out**.

\<aside><br />ℹ️

Any unsaved text in the composer is auto-saved before the note closes.

\</aside>

Screen Recording 2026-08-13 at 3.09.09 [PM.mov](http://PM.mov)

### Reopen a Care Note

**Steps:**

1. Open a Closed note.
2. Click **Reopen** in the drawer footer.
3. Confirm.

\<aside><br />⚠️

Reopening clears the assignee. A new task is not automatically created, so reassign manually if follow-up is still needed.

\</aside>

Screen Recording 2026-08-13 at 3.10.15 [PM.mov](http://PM.mov)

### Export a single closed Care Note

**Steps:**

1. Locate a Closed care note row.
2. Click the **Download** icon.
3. Review the PDF preview.

Screen Recording 2026-08-13 at 3.13.45 [PM.mov](http://PM.mov)

### Bulk download or fax Care Notes

**Steps:**

1. Select up to 25 closed care note rows using the checkboxes.
2. Click **Download PDF** or **Fax PDF** in the bulk action bar.
3. Complete the fax form if faxing.

Screen Recording 2026-08-13 at 3.14.44 [PM.mov](http://PM.mov)

### Archive and restore a Care Note

**Steps:**

1. On a Closed care note row, open **More (⋮)** then **Archive Care Note**.
2. Confirm.
3. Enable **Show Archived** in Filters to find it again.
4. Open **More (⋮)** then **Unarchive Care Note** to restore it.

## Important Behavior & Limitations

- A note entry is required to create a Care Note; all other fields are optional.
- Submitted entries are immutable. Once added, an entry cannot be edited.
- Only one existing patient attachment can be selected per note.
- Notes must be **Closed** before they can be exported or faxed.
- Dismissing the drawer with **X** or clicking the backdrop discards unsaved text. Use **Update Care Note**, or the footer's close action, to save first.
- Care Notes cannot be backdated or deleted. Closed notes can be archived instead, with a full audit trail.
- Bulk export/fax is capped at 25 notes at a time.
