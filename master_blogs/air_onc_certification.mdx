We're excited to share a major milestone: **Air is now ONC Health IT Certified.** Certification confirms that Air meets the federal standards for interoperability, security, and patient access set by the Office of the National Coordinator for Health Information Technology (ONC) — and it ships with a set of capabilities that make it easier for your practice to coordinate care, exchange records, and give patients real control over their health information.

_CHPL Product Number: [15.04.04.3268.Air1.01.00.1.260617](https://chpl.healthit.gov/#/listing/11864) · Certified June 17, 2026._

## What ONC Certification Means

The ONC Health IT Certification Program is the federal benchmark for electronic health record technology. To earn it, an EHR has to prove — through independent, third-party testing — that it can securely store health data, exchange it with other systems, and put it in patients' hands on demand.

That matters for your practice in three concrete ways:

- **You stay compliant with the 21st Century Cures Act.** Federal law now treats patient access to health information as the expected norm and prohibits "information blocking." Certified technology is how you meet that bar without building anything yourself.
- **You protect your Medicare reimbursement.** Programs like MIPS and Promoting Interoperability require Certified EHR Technology (CEHRT). Running on a certified platform keeps your practice eligible for incentives and out of penalty territory.
- **Your patients get a genuine right to their data.** Certification guarantees patients can view, download, and send their records — to a new provider, a caregiver, or an app of their choosing.

The complete list of Air's certified capabilities, costs, and any relied-upon software is published in our [Mandatory Disclosure](/air_developer/onc_certification/mandatory_disclosure).

## What's New in Air

Certification came with real features your team and patients can use today.

### 1. **Clinical Document Exchange**

![The C-CDA Continuity of Care Document viewer and inbox](/images/master_blogs/air_onc_certification/air_onc_certification_2.webp)

Send and receive standardized C-CDA clinical documents with any other provider, right from the patient's chart.

**What you can do:**

- Generate and send a complete clinical summary to a referring or receiving provider through Direct secure messaging
- Receive documents into a built-in inbox and read them in a clean, organized viewer
- Reconcile incoming problems, medications, and allergies straight into the patient's chart — no re-keying
- Match every incoming document to the right patient

This replaces faxing and manual data entry with structured, coded data that flows directly into the record.

### 2. **Patient & Caregiver Access to Records**

![Patients viewing and downloading records from the portal](/images/master_blogs/air_onc_certification/air_onc_certification_3.webp)

Patients can now view and download their own health records directly from the patient portal — and so can the people who help them.

**What you can do:**

- Let patients view, download, and transmit their health information to a third party of their choice
- Give caregivers, guardians, and authorized representatives their own portal sign-in to manage records, appointments, and forms on a patient's behalf
- Configure exactly what each person is allowed to see and do

### 3. **Electronic Health Information (EHI) Export**

Your team and your patients can export a complete, computable copy of a patient's record — on your terms, not a developer's timeline.

**What you can do:**

- Export a single patient's full clinical record as a C-CDA document, or their claims and encounter data as a CSV, yourself
- Request a population-level export of all patients when you need a full data set
- Move data cleanly for patient requests, audits, or transitions — you're never locked in

### 4. **Open FHIR API for the Apps You Choose**

Air provides a standardized FHIR R4 API (US Core 6.1.0, SMART App Launch 2.0) so the applications your practice and patients trust can securely connect to Air.

**What you can do:**

- Connect patient-authorized apps to read health data through a modern, standards-based API
- Power population-level tools with FHIR Bulk Data export
- Do it with no per-app, per-call, registration, or production fees

See the [Commure EHR FHIR API](/air_developer/fhir_api/commure_ehr_fhir_api) reference to get started.

## What This Means for Your Practice

**Compliance, handled.** Certification keeps you aligned with the Cures Act and eligible for MIPS and Promoting Interoperability — without extra work on your end.

**Care coordination without the fax machine.** Clinical Document Exchange turns referrals and records into structured data that lands directly in the chart.

**Patients in control.** View, download, transmit, and caregiver access give patients a real, federally-backed window into their own care.

**Your data stays yours.** EHI export and an open API mean you can move information whenever you need to.
