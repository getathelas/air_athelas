Any patient’s profile can be accessed by clicking on Patients under the EHR section and typing in the name and selecting the patient.

<img src="/images/insights_provider/patient_profiles/getting_started_with_patient_profile/getting_started_with_patient_profile_1.png" alt="" />

Selecting the patient’s name will take you to the patient’s profile- specifically to the Appointments tab. Here, you will see several other options: Demographics, Attachments, Tasks, Medications, Allergies, Vitals, Immunizations, and Labs

<img src="/images/insights_provider/patient_profiles/getting_started_with_patient_profile/getting_started_with_patient_profile_2.png" alt="" />

<img src="/images/insights_provider/patient_profiles/getting_started_with_patient_profile/getting_started_with_patient_profile_3.png" alt="" />

You can access pinned “sticky” notes for storing quick-view notes on patients

<img src="/images/insights_provider/patient_profiles/getting_started_with_patient_profile/getting_started_with_patient_profile_4.png" alt="" />

Demographics are covered under the Patient Demographics tab. For attachments, see [Add attachments to Patient Profile](/insights_provider/patient_profiles/add_attachments_to_patient_profile).

## Labs

- Send and manage electronic lab orders via HealthGorilla ([100+ supported lab vendors](https://developer.healthgorilla.com/docs/list-of-connected-labs)) for consultation and in-place specimen collection
- Receive lab results via PDF in the patient profile
- Attach ask-at-order questions to lab orders
- Download PDFs of labs

<img src="/images/insights_provider/patient_profiles/getting_started_with_patient_profile/getting_started_with_patient_profile_5.png" alt="Labs tab in the patient profile showing lab orders and results" />

## Features in development

- Store histories in the patient profile (ex. Surgical, Social, Family, etc)
- Patient activity timeline tracking chronological order of care events (ex. appointments scheduled, chart note signed, orders placed, lab results received, etc)
- Contact list for storing contact profiles linked to patients (ex. Attorney, Case Manager, Guardian, etc)

## Features not supported

- Multiple types of phone numbers and addresses (ex. home phone, cell phone, work address, home address)
- Categorize attachments into folders
- AI auto-tagging attachments
- Preview file before faxing
- See user logs and version history
