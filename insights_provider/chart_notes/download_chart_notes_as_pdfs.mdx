---
title: "Download Chart Notes as PDFs"
description: "Download, review, and fax completed chart notes as Billing or Plan of Care PDFs."
---

**Before you start:** Confirm that the chart note is complete.

Once a chart note is complete, you can find it in the **Appointments** section of the patient profile. Use the **Status** field to confirm the chart note stage.

<img src="/images/insights_provider/chart_notes/download_chart_notes_as_pdfs/download_chart_notes_as_pdfs_1.png" alt="Appointments section in a patient profile" />

<img src="/images/insights_provider/chart_notes/download_chart_notes_as_pdfs/download_chart_notes_as_pdfs_2.png" alt="Completed chart note with an available document preview" />

When the appointment is **Completed**, the eye icon changes from gray to black to show that the document is available. You can download a **Medical Billing PDF** or a **Plan of Care PDF**.

For an addended chart note, the original PDF is marked as addended. The latest PDF identifies the addendum at the top of the note instead of displaying an **ADDENDED** watermark across every page.

When a chart note uses Services, its Billing PDF displays the section as **Services** rather than **Treatments**.

After you download the chart note, the document depends on the PDF type you selected.

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Why does an older version of a chart note say ADDENDED?">
The label identifies the original note as a version that was later updated by an addendum. The latest PDF identifies the addendum at the top of the note.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which PDF types can I download?">
You can download a **Medical Billing PDF** or a **Plan of Care PDF** after the chart note is complete.
</Accordion>
