---
title: "Setting up Co-signers on Your Note"
---

If a note needs a **co-signature** from a referring provider, you can fax the encounter to them directly from the chart note.

## Add a co-signer

1. In the chart note, open the **co-signer** dropdown.
2. **Select the referring provider's name** from the list.
3. **Enter their fax number**.
4. Send the encounter to fax them the note for co-signature.
