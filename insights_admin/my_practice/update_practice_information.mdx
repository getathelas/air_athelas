---
title: "Update Practice Information"
---

Keep your practice's details and hours current from **Settings**.

## Update practice details

Go to **Settings → My Practice** and open the **Practice Details** tab to update your practice information.

<img src="/images/insights_admin/my_practice/update_practice_information/update_practice_information_1.png" alt="Practice Details tab under Settings, My Practice" />

## Set your business hours

Go to **Preferences → Calendar Settings** and select **Site** to set your practice's business hours.

<img src="/images/insights_admin/my_practice/update_practice_information/update_practice_information_2.png" alt="Site business hours configuration under Calendar Settings" />
