**Everything that used to take a back-and-forth with support, you can now do yourself.**

Change a billing rule. Reprice a fee schedule. Work a denial. Close your month. In Practice Manager,
you can do all of it directly.

It is the same platform and the same claims engine behind our fully-managed revenue cycle service,
with your team at the controls instead of ours. It is built for practices that already have a billing
team and would rather keep billing in their own hands.

The two sections below are the ones most people want first: what Athelas sets up, and what your team
runs. Everything after that is the catalog of what you get, labeled so you can tell what is generally
available from what is still in beta.

## Who configures what

Athelas pre-configures the billing rules that apply globally, plus the plumbing every practice needs.
You configure everything specific to your practice.

## What your team owns

Automation covers the standard path. These are the places your team stays in control:

- **Payers that only accept paper.** Some payers still require a mailed claim. You build it in
  Practice Manager the same way as any other, and your team mails it. If you would rather Athelas
  handle the mailing, that is a pass-through arrangement to set up with your Athelas team.
- **Eligibility runs through the clearinghouse.** When a payer is not reachable there, your team
  handles the call into the payer directly.
- **Prior authorization.** Eligibility tells you what is covered, not whether an authorization is
  required. Your team confirms that with the payer.
- **Workers' comp.** Registration and follow-up stay with your team, with first-class fields for them
  in the product rather than workarounds.
- **Anything outside automation is yours to work.** There is no Athelas back-office picking up the
  remainder, which is the point: nobody touches your claims but your team.
- **Patient billing questions come to you.** All inbound patient billing calls route to your team.

## How the platform is built

Practice Manager is not a pile of features. It is three pillars, and everything in the catalog below
is one of them.

### <Icon icon="wand-magic-sparkles" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={23} /> Automations

**Work less.** Your rules do the rote work, so your team doesn't.

Eligibility runs on every encounter rather than just initial evaluations. Patient responsibility is
calculated from live benefits before the patient arrives. Payment plans charge themselves once
configured. Deposits match themselves to the claims they actually paid.

Rules are grouped by what they act on, from building and modifying claims before submission to
posting remittances and taking adjustments. New ones stay as drafts until you review and turn them on.

![The Automations page, with rules grouped by type and drafts waiting for review](/images/practice_manager/overview/overview_1.webp)

### <Icon icon="table-columns" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={23} /> Workspaces

**Work fast.** One person acts on hundreds of claims at once.

Claims, denials, remittances, appointments, and patients, each with deep filters, saved views per
user, and bulk actions.

Every claim carries its status, stage, and owner on the same row, so a biller can tell what a claim is
waiting on without opening it. Assign and group claims by owner, and each biller works their own list.

![The Claims page, showing claim status, stage, and assignee across saved views](/images/practice_manager/overview/overview_2.webp)

### <Icon icon="chart-column" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={23} /> Analytics

**Work smart.** See what's working and where you can improve.

The throughput of your automations and your billing team, and the financial outcome of every month
of service. Every number opens to the claims behind it.

First-pass rate, collection rate, time to decision, and denial rate all sit on one screen, each
compared against the prior period so a trend is visible without building a report.

![The Practice Insights dashboard, with first-pass rate, collection rate, denial rate, and historical payments](/images/practice_manager/overview/overview_3.webp)

**Billing work is shared across all three.** Claims can be assigned to a specific biller, and the
Athelas Assistant reads your live billing data and acts on a single claim or every claim you have
selected.
