**Everything that used to take a back-and-forth with support, you can now do yourself.**

Change a billing rule. Reprice a fee schedule. Work a denial. Close your month. In Practice Manager,
you can do all of it directly.

It is the same platform and the same claims engine behind our fully-managed revenue cycle service,
with your team at the controls instead of ours. Because your team does the work, Practice Manager
costs less and scales with you rather than with us.

This section is the catalog of what you get. Where a capability has a guide, it is linked in the Docs
column; we are still writing up the newer ones.

## How the platform is built

Practice Manager is not a pile of features. It is three primitives, and everything in the catalog below
is one of them.

### <Icon icon="wand-magic-sparkles" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={23} /> Automations

Where you take control of the rules and data constants that eliminate rote work.

Eligibility runs on every encounter rather than just initial evaluations. Patient responsibility is
calculated from live benefits before the patient arrives. Payment plans charge themselves once
configured. Deposits match themselves to the claims they actually paid.

Rules are grouped by what they act on, from building and modifying claims before submission through
posting remittances and taking adjustments. New ones stay as drafts until you review and promote them.

![The Automations page, with rules grouped by type and drafts waiting for review](/images/practice_manager/overview/overview_1.webp)

### <Icon icon="table-columns" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={23} /> Workspaces

Where your team does the work that needs human judgement.

Claims, denials, remittances, appointments, and patients, each with deep filters, saved views per
user, and bulk actions that let one person act on hundreds of claims at once.

Every claim carries its status, stage, and owner on the same row, so a biller can tell what a claim is
waiting on without opening it.

![The Claims page, showing claim status, stage, and assignee across saved views](/images/practice_manager/overview/overview_2.webp)

### <Icon icon="chart-column" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={23} /> Analytics

Where you see whether any of it is working.

The throughput of your automations and your billing team, and the financial outcome of every month
of service. Every number opens to the claims behind it.

First-pass rate, collection rate, time to decision, and denial rate all sit on one screen, each
compared against the prior period so a trend is visible without building a report.

![The Practice Insights dashboard, with first-pass rate, collection rate, denial rate, and historical payments](/images/practice_manager/overview/overview_3.webp)

**Collaboration runs across all three.** Tasks, messages, and an assistant that reads your live billing
data and can act on a single claim or every claim you have selected.

one-time onboarding and training during launch.

## What you'll still do manually

Automation covers the standard path. These are the places your team picks it up:

- **Eligibility runs through the clearinghouse.** If a payer is not reachable there, someone on your
  team calls them directly.
- **Eligibility does not tell you whether prior authorization is required.** Confirm that with the
  payer.
- **Workers' comp registration and follow-up is manual.**
- **Anything outside automation is yours to work.** There is no Athelas back-office picking up the
  remainder.
- **Patient billing questions come to you.** There is no two-way patient messaging in Insights, and
  an Athelas inbound patient phone line is part of the managed service rather than Practice Manager.
