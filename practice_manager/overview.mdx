**The engine behind our managed RCM service. Your team at the controls.**

Practice Manager is the same platform and the same engine behind our fully managed revenue cycle service, with your team at the controls. It is built for practices that already have a billing team and want to run it on a smoother, more customized platform.

## Who configures what

Athelas pre-configures the billing rules that apply globally, plus the plumbing every practice needs. You configure everything specific to your practice.

Note that our Athelas team runs one-time onboarding and training during launch, so none of this starts from a blank page.

## What stays with your team

Nobody outside your practice touches a claim; the queue is yours end to end, which means:

- **Patients talk to you.** Every inbound billing call routes to your team, so the person answering knows the practice and the patient.
- **Prior authorization is your call.** Eligibility tells you what's covered; whether an authorization is required takes a conversation with the payer, and your team owns it.
- **Workers' comp stays in-house.** Registration and follow-up are yours, with first-class fields in the product rather than workarounds.
- **Paper payers work like any other.** A few payers still require a mailed claim. You build it in Practice Manager exactly as you would an electronic one, and your team drops it in the mail.
- **Eligibility gaps route to you.** Eligibility runs through the clearinghouse. When a payer isn't reachable there, your team makes the call directly.

## How the platform is built

Practice Manager rests on three pillars. Every capability we offer belongs to one of them.

### <Icon icon="wand-magic-sparkles" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={23} /> Automations

![The Automations page, with rules grouped by type and drafts waiting for review](/images/practice_manager/overview/overview_1.webp)

**Work less.** Your rules do the rote work, so your team can focus on what's more important.

Eligibility runs on every encounter rather than just initial evaluations. Patient responsibility is calculated from live benefits before the patient arrives. Payment plans charge themselves once configured. Deposits match themselves to the claims they actually paid.

Rules are grouped by what they act on, from building and modifying claims before submission to posting remittances and taking adjustments. New ones stay as drafts until you review and turn them on.

### <Icon icon="table-columns" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={23} /> Workspaces

![The Claims page, showing claim status, stage, and assignee across saved views](/images/practice_manager/overview/overview_2.webp)

**Work fast.** One person can now act on hundreds of claims at once.

Claims, denials, remittances, appointments, and patients, each with deep filters, saved views per user, and bulk actions.

Every claim carries its status, stage, and owner on the same row, so a biller can tell what a claim is waiting on without opening it. Assign and group claims by owner so each biller works their own list.

### <Icon icon="chart-column" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={23} /> Analytics

![The Practice Insights dashboard, with first-pass rate, collection rate, denial rate, and historical payments](/images/practice_manager/overview/overview_3.webp)

**Work smart.** See what's working and where you can improve.

The throughput of your automations and your billing team, and the financial outcome of every month of service. Every number opens to the claims behind it.

First-pass rate, collection rate, time to decision, and denial rate all sit on one screen, each compared against the prior period so a trend is visible without building a report.
