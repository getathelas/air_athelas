This is the engine room. The rules engine encodes your billing logic once so it applies to every claim
automatically, and the claims workspace is where your team works everything the rules could not finish
on their own. Rules are yours to author, test, and roll back.

<Icon icon="flask" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={16} /> **Beta.** In product and working, but ask your Athelas team to turn it on.

| **AI rule generation** <Icon icon="flask" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={16} /> | Describe what you want in plain language and get a drafted rule, including complex billing and claim-transformation logic. | [Create a Rule](/insights_biller/automations/create_a_rule) |
| **Rule impact preview** <Icon icon="flask" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={16} /> | The claims and appointments a rule would affect, before you launch it. | [Create a Rule](/insights_biller/automations/create_a_rule) |
| **Rule audit trail & rollback** | Every version is kept, with one-click restore. | [The Rules Tab](/insights_biller/automations/the_rules_tab) |
| **Plain-language rule view** | A human-readable summary sits next to each rule. AI explains even regex-heavy conditions, and internal IDs resolve to real payer, provider, and patient names. | [The Rules Tab](/insights_biller/automations/the_rules_tab) |
| **Test a rule against past claims** <Icon icon="flask" iconType="duotone" color="#F9345F" size={16} /> | A before-and-after comparison of what the rule would have changed. One rule at a time. | |
| **New-rule safety sampling** | An automatic platform safeguard: new rules apply to a limited sample of claims first, then expand once performance is confirmed. Applies to billing rules and auto write-off rules alike, and sampling progress is visible in product. | |
