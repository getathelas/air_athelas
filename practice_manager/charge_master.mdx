Your fee schedule is the single source of truth for what you bill. The Charge Master gives you direct
control over it per payer, per facility, and on a schedule, with a full audit trail behind every
change.

| Capability | What it does | Included in | Docs |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Charge Master editor** | Full fee-schedule control with bulk edits and live preview. | PM | [Getting Started with Charge Master](/insights_biller/general_billing/getting_started_with_charge_master) |
| **Facility-specific pricing** | Facility rates win over site defaults. | PM | [Getting Started with Charge Master](/insights_biller/general_billing/getting_started_with_charge_master) |
| **Payer mapping** | Payer-specific rows with the best match picked at claim time, and every messy payer-name variant mapped to one canonical payer. | PM | [Getting Started with Charge Master](/insights_biller/general_billing/getting_started_with_charge_master) |
| **Future-dated changes** | Prices flip live on schedule, with no overlaps. | PM | [Getting Started with Charge Master](/insights_biller/general_billing/getting_started_with_charge_master) |
| **Multi-facility transfer** | Copy charges across facilities, as-is or scaled. | PM | [Getting Started with Charge Master](/insights_biller/general_billing/getting_started_with_charge_master) |
| **Charge audit history** | Every change, with before and after values, who made it, and when. | PM | [Getting Started with Charge Master](/insights_biller/general_billing/getting_started_with_charge_master) |
| **Self-pay fee schedule** | A separate cash-pay price list alongside your insurance rates, so uninsured and self-pay patients are quoted the right number at the counter without touching payer pricing. | PM | [Self-pay Fee Schedule](/insights_front_desk/front_office_payments/self_pay_fee_schedule) |
| **Summary of Benefits** | An auto-built PDF summarizing a patient's verified benefits: copay, coinsurance, deductible, and out-of-pocket maximum. | PM |  |
