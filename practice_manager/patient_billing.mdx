Once the payer has adjudicated, whatever is left belongs to the patient. This phase covers calculating
that number correctly, explaining it clearly, and making it easy to pay, on a schedule you control
and through whichever channel the patient actually responds to.

| Capability | What it does | Included in | Docs |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Patient responsibility (PR) generation** | Auto-calculates what the patient owes (pre-visit from eligibility, or post-visit from the adjudicated balance) and creates the PR charge, applying rules like preventive-visit waivers and sliding-fee schedules. | PM | [Patient Responsibility Page](/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/patient_responsibility_page) |
| **Recurring patient statements** | Your cadence (interval, anchor, skip-days, pause), targeting only patients with a real balance. Three channels (text, email, mail), branded, with a pay link on every one, smart-batched in the patient's timezone, deduplicated, and double-send guarded. | PM | [Manage Patient Statements](/insights_front_desk/patient_statements/manage_patient_statements) |
| **Send a single statement** | One-off statement to one patient, on any channel. | PM | [Send Patient Statements via Email/Text](/insights_front_desk/patient_statements/send_patient_statements_via_emailtext) |
| **SmartPay hosted payments** | One-tap pay page, code-verified, supporting card, bank debit, saved card, gift card, and credit, with check and cash mail-back also supported. Payments post instantly and are allocated to the exact items they satisfy. | PM | [How to Send a Patient Payment Link](/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/how_to_send_a_patient_payment_link) |
| **Payment plans & patient subscriptions** | Fixed or pay-down plans auto-charged on schedule, plus recurring subscription-style charges such as memberships and wellness plans, tracked per patient. | PM | [Setting up a Payment Plan](/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/setting_up_a_payment_plan) |
| **PR breakdown** | A full per-visit breakdown for billers covering billed, paid, adjusted, credits, PR, refunds, and remaining, with payment-lifecycle and remittance timelines linked to the remit PDF. Patients see the distilled version on the statement and pay page. | PM | [PR Timeline](/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/pr_timeline) |
| **Manage contact info** | Address, phone, and email in one place. Addresses are auto-validated and standardized, and every change is tracked. | PM | [Getting Started with Demographics](/insights_front_desk/patient_demographics/getting_started_with_demographics) |
| **Failed-plan-charge detection** | Failed payment-plan charges are detected and recorded. | PM |  |
| **PR Explainability** | A charge-level explanation of exactly why a patient owes what they owe. | Beta |  |
| **Manage financial guarantor** | Responsible-party setup with duplicate detection, reusable across patients (one parent, several children) and linked to the insurance policy, with full change history. | PM |  |
| **Gift cards** | Pre-funded credits with a unique code and a tracked running balance, droppable into a statement as a pay-now incentive. | PM |  |
