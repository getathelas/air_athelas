which providers are credentialed with which payers, how your calendar is structured, and what you
bill. Get this right and the rest of the revenue cycle behaves predictably.

## Charge Master

Your fee schedule is the single source of truth for what you bill. The Charge Master gives you direct
control over it per payer, per facility, and on a schedule, with a full audit trail behind every
change.
