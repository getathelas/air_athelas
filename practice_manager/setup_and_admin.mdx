Everything you configure once, before the first claim goes out: who can do what, where you practice,
which providers are credentialed with which payers, and how your calendar is structured. Get this
right and the rest of the revenue cycle behaves predictably.

| Capability | What it does | Included in | Docs |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Staff & permission management (RBAC)** | Roles per site with page-level *and* action-level permission gates for refunds, write-offs, claim edits, and user management. Scope access by site, facility, or facility group, and every change is logged. | PM | [Manage Staff & Permissions](/insights_admin/my_practice/manage_staff_permissions) |
| **Practice settings** | Locations, hours, and facilities. | PM | [Update Practice Information](/insights_admin/my_practice/update_practice_information) |
| **Provider credentialing** | Payer enrollment and credentialing status per provider in one place: what is approved, what is pending, and what is blocking a provider from billing a given payer. | PM | [Provider Credentials](/air_admin/manage_your_practice/provider_credentials) |
| **Provider settings & e-Rx registration** | Provider configuration and Surescripts enrollment. | PM | [Provider Settings](/air_admin/manage_your_practice/provider_settings) |
| **Appointment type & calendar setup** | Appointment types, templates, and provider hours. | PM | [Appointment Types](/air_admin/manage_your_practice/appointment_types) |
| **Athelas Assistant** | AI copilot that reads your live billing data and answers plain-English questions, produces claim and denial summaries with next steps, and runs the EOB copilot. | Beta | [Getting Started with Athelas Assistant](/insights_general/athelas_assistant/getting_started_with_athelas_assistant) |
| **EDI & ERA enrollments** | Every provider registered with every payer across our clearinghouses, tracked per payer, NPI, and TIN, and auto-confirmed the moment your first live claim or remittance flows. Handled by Athelas at onboarding. | PM |  |
| **Stripe account & POS setup** | Athelas sets up your Stripe account for cards, ACH, and Affirm, and gets card readers ordered, registered, and live at the front desk. | PM |  |
| **Coaching & training** | One-time onboarding and training run with your Athelas team during launch. | PM |  |
