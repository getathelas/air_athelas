#### At a Glance

The [Billing Rules Engine page](https://insights.athelas.com/v2/rules-engine) lists all active and inactive billing rules implemented by a collaborative effort between your organization, an Athelas account manager, and our engineers.

The two types of rules found here are categorized as Global or Site. More on that later in this guide.

**To make changes to rules, contact your account manager**.

###

### The Billing Rules Process

Early in the Athelas onboarding process for your organization, an account manager collaborated with your organization’s administrators to incorporate all existing billing rules, updates to those rules, and any new rules requested into Insights.

These rules, along with any added manually since then, comprise the list visible on the Billing Rules Engine page.

You can filter this list to show only Active, Inactive, or All.

#### Types of Rules

- **Global Rules:** Standardized rules available to organizations using Athelas Insights. These rules address common claim adjustments.
- **Site Rules:** Rules specific to your site.

<img src="/images/insights_biller/general_billing/the_billing_rules_engine/the_billing_rules_engine_3.png" alt="Global and site billing rule tabs" />

The explanatory notes on these are editable. Click the ‘Edit’ button below the explanation to make changes.

<img src="/images/insights_biller/general_billing/the_billing_rules_engine/the_billing_rules_engine_4.png" alt="Editable explanatory notes for a billing rule" />

#### Code for Site Rules

Scroll to the right in the Site Rules tab and you can see the trigger for each rule, the actions subsequently applied, and who created the rule.

This is the first place to check if a site rule is failing in some way.


#### View Active Rules on a Claim

To see which rules were applied to an individual claim, visit the [Claim Details page](https://insights.athelas.com/v3/claim_level_view). Click into a claim, then switch the the ‘Submissions’ tab. Choose a submission to expand and click ‘x rules applied.’

#### Review rule details

Open a rule to review its conditions, actions, and properties. For rule types that support phased rollout, **Rollout Cap** shows the current limit with thousands separators. **Fully Released** means that the supported rule type has no enforced cap. Rule types that do not support phased rollout do not show this property.

Select **Dry Run** to test a rule and review its results before relying on it. Results replace the details view and use one tab per tested entity. Conditions can show **Met**, **Not met**, or **Skipped**, and actions can show **Applied**. Close the results to return to the rule details.

### FAQ

For a rule type that supports phased rollout, **Fully Released** means the rule has no enforced rollout cap.
</Accordion>

No. Contact your account manager to request a rule change.
