Use this guide when uploading EOBs with **EOB Copilot** on the [Remittances → EOBs](https://insights.athelas.com/v2/eob-posting) page. Following these practices improves check creation, claim matching, and transcription quality.

## Quick rules

- Upload **one check at a time**, or multiple PDFs that share the **same EOB format**.
- Do **not** use EOB Copilot for **correspondence**, portal letters, or other non-EOB documents.
- Avoid bulk uploads that **mix formats**, include correspondence, or lack **procedure-level** payment detail.

<Warning>
Bulk uploads that mix document types or formats may miss claims, skip transcription, or produce incomplete checks.
</Warning>

## Good uploads

These uploads work well with EOB Copilot:

- A single remittance advice PDF for **one check** (one payer payment).
- Consistent layout across files when uploading multiple PDFs in one batch (same payer/format).
- Clear **procedure-level** lines (CPT/HCPCS, paid amounts, adjustments) per claim.

## Uploads to avoid

Do **not** upload these through EOB Copilot:

- Payer **correspondence** (denial letters, requests for records, general mail).
- Bundles that combine **multiple unrelated checks** or **multiple EOB formats**.
- EOBs with **no procedure-level breakdown** (summary-only pages without line-item detail).
