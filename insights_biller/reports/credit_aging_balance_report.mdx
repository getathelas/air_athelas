title: "Credit Aging Balance Report"

#### Summary

The **Credit Aging Balance Report** identifies patients who have an unapplied credit balance on their account — meaning more money has been collected from them than is currently owed. For each patient, the report shows the actionable credit amount, any remaining outstanding balance, open-claim and upcoming-appointment signals, and whether the patient is eligible for a refund. Patient, address, and guarantor details are included to support mailing refund checks directly from the report.

The **Amount in Credits** figure is driven by three kinds of credit:

- **Flexible credits** — not tied to a specific date of service; always safe to consider for refund.
- **Held credits** — tied to a date of service whose encounters are all finalized; safe to refund.
- **Locked credits** — tied to a date of service that still has open encounters; excluded from Amount in Credits until adjudication completes.

Only patients with a positive flexible + held credit balance appear. Patients whose entire balance is locked do not appear.

<Note>
**Refund eligibility:** A patient is marked **Refund Eligible = Yes** when all of their encounters have reached a finalized adjudication status AND they have no remaining outstanding patient responsibility. The upcoming-appointment flag is informational — it does not block eligibility, but signals that credits may be needed for a future visit.
</Note>

#### Key Use Cases

- Identify patients who may be owed a refund and prioritize by credit amount.
- Use the patient and guarantor address columns to prepare refund checks without a separate lookup.
- Flag patients with upcoming appointments so staff can decide whether to hold or refund the credit.
- Resolve credit balances before they age into compliance or patient-satisfaction issues.
- Support month-end balance cleanup and refund processing.

#### Data Dictionary

| Column Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Patient ID | Internal patient identifier. |
| Patient Name | Full name of the patient (hidden in the shared report view). |
| Patient Profile Link | Direct deep-link to the patient's details page in Insights, pre-scoped to the site. |
| Patient Address | Mailing address of the patient, formatted as a single line. Useful for mailing refund checks (hidden in the shared report view). |
| Guarantor Name | Full name of the guarantor (responsible party) on the patient's insurance record. Blank if no guarantor is on file (hidden in the shared report view). |
| Guarantor Address | Mailing address of the guarantor, formatted as a single line. Blank if no guarantor or address is on file (hidden in the shared report view). |
| Amount in Credits | The actionable credit balance for this patient (\$) — sum of flexible credits (no date of service) and held credits (date of service finalized). Excludes locked credits. This is the amount that is safe to refund or apply. |
| Outstanding Payment | Total outstanding patient responsibility still owed by this patient (\$). If non-zero, a refund would be premature. |
| Claims Waiting for Adjudication | "Yes" if the patient has any encounters still being processed by a payer; "No" if all claims have cleared the payer. |
| Upcoming Appointment (Next 30 Days) | "Yes" if the patient has a scheduled or confirmed appointment within the next 30 days. Informational only. |
| Refund Eligible | "Yes" if the patient has no open encounters and no outstanding patient responsibility; "No" otherwise. Rows with Refund Eligible = Yes appear first, sorted by credit amount descending. |

#### Filters Supported

- No run-time filters. The report returns a current snapshot of all patients with an actionable credit balance.
