This guide walks your financial and accounting team through month-end close in Insights. It covers the Month End reports in **My Reports**, how cash moves through the states of posting, and which general ledger (GL) entries to book from each report, whether you are on cash-basis or accrual accounting. Review the [Glossary](#glossary) to get familiar with the terms used throughout the guide.

## The states of posting

At any moment, every dollar that reaches your bank is in one of the following states, and each report owns one slice.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/reports/month_end_close/month_end_close_1.svg"
  alt="The cash formula: total bank deposits split into unposted payer money, unapplied and free patient credits, applied payments, and miscellaneous payments, each with the report where you see it"
  title="The cash formula"
  style={{ width:"100%" }}
/>

| **State of posting** | **What it means** | **Where to see it** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Unposted payer money** | Payer cash in your bank that has not been applied to a claim yet | [Bank Deposit Reconciliation Report](/insights_biller/reports/bank_deposit_reconciliation_report) |
| **Unapplied and free patient credits** | Patient money you are holding that has not been applied to a balance or that can be refunded | [Patient Credits Report](/insights_biller/reports/patient_credits_report) |
| **Applied or misc payments** | Payments matched and posted to a specific encounter or miscellaneous charge | [Encounter Revenue Activity Report](/insights_biller/reports/encounter_revenue_activity_report)<br />[Misc Revenue Activity Report](/insights_biller/reports/misc_revenue_activity_report) |

<Warning>
**Know your lockbox setup.** If Athelas receives your lockbox, the Bank Deposit Reconciliation Report is a complete view of insurance cash. If not, the report may be incomplete. Use your **bank statement as the source of truth for cash**, and use the report as a reconciliation tool to make sure Insights has every deposit it needs for posting.
</Warning>

## How money moves between the states

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/reports/month_end_close/month_end_close_2.svg"
  alt="Diagram of payer and patient cash entering Unposted Money by deposit date and leaving by date posted into applied payments, miscellaneous payments, and patient refund liability"
  title="How money moves into and out of Unposted Money"
  style={{ width:"100%" }}
/>

Cash enters on the **deposit date** and leaves on the **date posted**. Whatever has not crossed by the last day of the month is your Unposted Money balance, the account that makes cash and posted payments reconcile.

For the full explanation, see [Understanding Unposted Money](/insights_biller/reports/month_end_unposted_money).

## The month-end report suite

Five reports work together to give you a complete, auditable picture of your month. Each one is **drift-free**: re-running a closed month always returns the same numbers.

| **Report** | **The question it answers** | **Role in your close** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| [**Encounter Revenue Activity Report**](/insights_biller/reports/encounter_revenue_activity_report) | What encounter revenue did I earn, collect, and adjust? | Book charges, payments, adjustments, and AR, plus net revenue estimates where enabled |
| [**Misc Revenue Activity Report**](/insights_biller/reports/misc_revenue_activity_report) | What did I earn outside of claims (products, fees, programs)? | Book miscellaneous revenue |
| [**Provider Adjustments Report**](/insights_biller/reports/provider_adjustments_report) | What did payers add to or withhold from their checks outside of claims? | Book payer interest as miscellaneous revenue and forward balances as payer credits, so deposits tie to posted payments |
| [**Bank Deposit Reconciliation Report**](/insights_biller/reports/bank_deposit_reconciliation_report) | What cash actually hit my bank, and how much of it is posted? | Book cash by deposit date and reconcile to your bank statement |
| [**Patient Credits Report**](/insights_biller/reports/patient_credits_report) | What patient money am I holding that hasn't been applied? | Book refund-eligible credits to a patient refund liability (accrual) or refund expense (cash) |

## Before you start

- **Know your basis.** Accrual practices book AR, charges, and allowances; cash-basis practices book revenue when cash is received. The methodology differs, but the reports support both when you use them correctly.
- **Know your lockbox setup.** It decides whether your bank statement or the Bank Deposit Reconciliation Report is your source of truth for cash (see [The states of posting](#the-states-of-posting)).
- **Confirm your posting behavior.** Review your practice settings to confirm whether deposit verification is enabled. This setting changes when payments post, and therefore what appears as unposted at month-end.
- **Pick your dimensions.** Common summaries use drift-free dimensions such as provider, facility, payer group, and CPT code. For the full list of drift-free dimensions, see the [Encounter Revenue Activity Report](/insights_biller/reports/encounter_revenue_activity_report).
- **Understand Unposted Money.** If this is one of your first closes in Insights, read [Understanding Unposted Money](/insights_biller/reports/month_end_unposted_money) first. Unposted Money is the account that absorbs the difference between your cash and your posted payments, and practices find it the most unfamiliar concept in the close.

## Pick your track

The entry tables live on two separate pages, so you only need to read the one that applies to you:

- [**Accrual-Basis Entries**](/insights_biller/reports/month_end_accrual_entries), for practices that book accounts receivable.
- [**Cash-Basis Entries**](/insights_biller/reports/month_end_cash_entries), for practices that recognize revenue when cash is received.

## The close, step by step

1. **Run the reports** for the closed month: Encounter Revenue Activity, Misc Revenue Activity, Provider Adjustments, Bank Deposit Reconciliation, and Patient Credits. Start and end dates are inclusive on every report.
2. **Book cash by deposit date** from your bank statement (non-lockbox) or the Bank Deposit Reconciliation Report (lockbox).
3. **Book revenue activity by date posted** from the Encounter Revenue Activity Report. Accrual practices also book charges, adjustments, and AR movement here.
4. **Book miscellaneous revenue** from the Misc Revenue Activity Report, plus **Payer Aux Cash** from the Provider Adjustments Report.
5. **Book payer credits:** the net change in the **Payer Credit Account** from the Provider Adjustments Report. If these are material for you, see [forward balances and payer credits](/insights_biller/reports/provider_adjustments_report).
6. **Book patient refunds** from the **Flexible** credits in the Patient Credits Report, to a liability on accrual or an expense on cash basis.
7. **Materially reconcile your Unposted Money account.** Its ending balance should represent your unposted cash (see [Understanding Unposted Money](/insights_biller/reports/month_end_unposted_money)).
8. **Tie posted insurance to Encounter Revenue Activity.** Check that the insurance cash posted this month in the Bank Deposit Reconciliation Report agrees with insurance **Paid** in the Encounter Revenue Activity Report (see the section below).
9. **Roll AR forward and file the reports** as audit support for your summarized entries.

## Materially reconcile your Unposted Money account

At month-end, the balance should represent the money in your bank that has not been applied yet:

```txt
Unposted Money  ≈   Unposted cash from Bank Deposit Reconciliation
                +   Patient credit balances (Patient Credits Report)
                +   Payer credit balance (Provider Adjustments Report),
                    if you do not keep a separate account
```

<Warning>
**Material support, not a penny-perfect tie-out.** A 100% match from your Unposted Money account to Insights reporting is not realistically achievable. Normal timing differences between workflow handoffs and data differences between systems (for example, activity that reaches your bank without ever reaching Insights) both get in the way. Support the balance materially and investigate the variances that matter. The full method and its current limitations are on [Understanding Unposted Money](/insights_biller/reports/month_end_unposted_money).
</Warning>

## Tie posted insurance to Encounter Revenue Activity

Insurance cash posted in the month should agree between the Bank Deposit Reconciliation Report and the Encounter Revenue Activity Report, once you remove the provider adjustments that aren't linked to an encounter.

```txt
Encounter Revenue Activity Paid, Payer Index 1–3
    =   Bank Deposit Reconciliation, run with Date Type = Date Posted:
            matched_posted_usd
          + unmatched_posted_usd
          + provider_level_adjustment_posted_usd
    −   Provider Adjustments Report, non-encounter adjustments only:
            Total Amount (USD) where Account Type is
            Payer Aux Cash, Payer Credit Account or Payer Credits Untied
```

**To tie out posted insurance:**

1. Run the [**Bank Deposit Reconciliation Report**](/insights_biller/reports/bank_deposit_reconciliation_report) with **Date Type** set to **Date Posted** for the closed month. Add up `matched_posted_usd`, `unmatched_posted_usd`, and `provider_level_adjustment_posted_usd`.
2. Run the [**Provider Adjustments Report**](/insights_biller/reports/provider_adjustments_report) for the same dates with only **Include Non-Encounter Provider Adjustments** selected. Sum **Total Amount (USD)** for the rows whose **Account Type** is **Payer Aux Cash**, **Payer Credit Account**, or **Payer Credits Untied**.
3. Subtract the step 2 total from the step 1 total.
4. Run the [**Encounter Revenue Activity Report**](/insights_biller/reports/encounter_revenue_activity_report) for the same dates and sum **Paid** for **Payer Index** 1, 2, and 3. That sum should equal the step 3 result.

**Why the subtraction:** when the Bank Deposit Reconciliation Report runs by date posted, `provider_level_adjustment_posted_usd` includes both encounter-linked and non-encounter provider adjustments, while Encounter Revenue Activity carries only the encounter-linked ones. You book non-encounter adjustments, such as payer interest and forward balances, from the Provider Adjustments Report instead. **Payer Credits Untied** rows and their matching **Payer Credit Account** rows net to zero, so including all three account types keeps the total right.

<Warning>
**Don't stop at `matched_posted_usd`.** Comparing `matched_posted_usd` alone, or the three posted columns without removing non-encounter provider adjustments, will not tie to Encounter Revenue Activity in any month when your payers sent interest, forward balances, or other check-level adjustments.
</Warning>

## Suggested accounts

| **Account** | **Type** | **Fed by** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Cash** | Asset | Bank deposits (by deposit date) |
| **Unposted Money** (titled Unapplied Revenue on many cash-basis charts of accounts) | Contra-asset (accrual) or contra-revenue (cash) | All cash in; relieved as payments post |
| **Accounts Receivable (AR)** | Asset | Charges, payments, adjustments, and transfers (accrual only) |
| **Gross Revenue** | Revenue | Charges (accrual) or paid amounts (cash basis) |
| **Contractual Allowance** | Contra-revenue | Contractual adjustments and estimates |
| **Miscellaneous Revenue** | Revenue | Misc Revenue Activity plus Payer Aux Cash |
| **Payer Credits** | Contra-AR / balance sheet | Provider Adjustments (Payer Credit Account) |
| **Patient Refund Liability** (accrual) / **Patient Refund Expense** (cash) | Liability / Expense | Patient Credits (Flexible) |

## Resolve unposted and unmatched deposits

After you run the Bank Deposit Reconciliation Report, review any amounts in `matched_unposted_usd` or `unmatched_usd` to decide whether anything needs action before you close the month.

### Matched but unposted: get a remittance posted

**What this means:** Insights linked the remittance (check or ERA) to a bank deposit but has not posted it to the ledger. This happens because the remittance was automatically placed into **Manual Review**, a guardrail that keeps the ledger from going out of balance.

**To resolve a matched, unposted remittance:**

1. In Insights, go to the **Claims** page, filter for **Unposted Remittances**, and select the status **Manual Review**.
2. Click into the claim. The **Encounter Stage** shows "Unposted Remittances."
3. In the **Posting Tool**, expand the procedure columns to find the specific remittance in Manual Review.
4. **Option A: Post it.**
   - Click the **⋯** (three dots) to the right of the payment item, select **Negate**, and enter a reason.
   - Review the payment preview, which shows the balance rollups.
   - Click **Post** in the upper right. The remittance is now posted to the ledger.
5. **Option B: Archive it** (if the remittance should not hit the ledger).
   - Click **Archive**. The line grays out and shows "Archived." The posting status updates to "Finalized," and the Unposted Remittances substage disappears from the encounter.

**Common reasons a remittance lands in Manual Review:**

- Conflicting PR decisions (for example, a resubmission where two remits show different patient-responsibility amounts).
- Data-integrity issues (the remittance doesn't balance to the charges on the ERA).
- A future check date (Insights holds posting until the check date has passed and the deposit is confirmed).
- The primary payer remit contains an `OA23` denial code, which indicates the impact of a prior payer's adjudication.

<Note>
For detailed posting guidance, see [How to Use the Posting Tool Page](/insights_front_desk/posting/how_to_use_the_posting_tool_page) and [How to Handle Duplicate Remittances](/insights_front_desk/posting/how_to_handle_duplicate_remittances).
</Note>

### Unmatched: get a check matched

**What this means:** Insights has a remittance (check or ERA), but it has not been linked to a corresponding deposit in your bank account.

**To match a check to its deposit:**

1. Go to **Daily Operations → Remittances** in the left navigation.
2. Click the **Checks** tab and apply the filter **Matching Status = Unmatched** to see all unmatched checks.
3. Click the unmatched check row.
4. Click **Deposit → Search Deposits**. Locate the matching deposit by Deposit ID, Date, Amount, Bank Account, Deposit Method, or Matching Status.
5. Once you find it, check the deposit and click **Link**. The check and deposit are now matched, and the amount moves from `unmatched_usd` to `matched_posted_usd` (or to `matched_unposted_usd` if posting is still pending).

**Common reasons a check may not auto-match:**

- The ERA was received before the check hit the bank (timing lag).
- A paper check was deposited after the remittance was received.
- Multiple checks were bundled into one deposit.
- A lockbox processing delay, or a weekend or bank-holiday lag.
- The payer batch was delayed.

<Note>
For the full bank-linking workflow, see [Bank Deposit Verification](/insights_front_desk/utilities/bank_deposit_verification).
</Note>

### Unmatched: you cannot find the deposit in Insights

If you search in Insights and the deposit does not appear anywhere, **the bank deposit has not yet been ingested into Insights.** This most commonly happens with paper checks processed through a lockbox, or with ACH deposits not yet captured in the BAI2 file feed.

**To handle a deposit that Insights hasn't ingested:**

1. Confirm the deposit appears in your bank account. If the deposit isn't there yet, wait 1–2 business days, because the gap may be a timing difference.
2. If the deposit is in the bank but absent from Insights, contact your Athelas team to ingest the deposit manually. Provide the **bank account name**, **deposit date**, **deposit amount**, and the **bank reference or trace number**.
3. **Do not hold the month open waiting for the match.** Best practice is to record the cash to Unposted Money at period close, then resolve the match in the next cycle once the deposit is ingested.
4. After the deposit is ingested into Insights, return to the **Remittances** page and link the check manually, using the steps in [Unmatched: get a check matched](#unmatched-get-a-check-matched).

## Glossary

| **Term** | **What it means** |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Accounts Receivable (AR)** | Money owed to you for charges raised but not yet paid or adjusted off. Shortened to AR throughout this guide. |
| **Unposted Money** | Cash that has reached your bank, or been recognized from a remittance, but has not been applied to a claim yet. It sits in a clearing account on your ledger. This guide uses this term consistently. |
| **Applied payment** | A payment matched and posted to a specific encounter, relieving that account's balance. |
| **AR transfer** | Movement of a balance from one payer to another. It has no effect on the encounter's total AR, only on AR by payer. |
| **Provider adjustment (PLB)** | A line on a remittance that changes the check total without referencing a specific claim. Examples include auxiliary fees, interest payments, and forward-applied balances. |
| **Payer auxiliary cash** | Payer money with no claim attached, such as interest, late-payment fees, and incentive payments. It is treated as miscellaneous revenue and is part of PLB. |
| **Forward balance** | A payer withholding part of today's payment and settling the withheld amount on a future remittance. It is part of PLB. |
| **Deposit date vs. date posted** | The deposit date is when cash hit your bank; the date posted is when the payment was applied in Insights. Cash entries use the deposit date, and revenue entries use the date posted. |
| **Drift-free** | A dimension whose value is locked at posting time, so re-running a closed month always returns the same numbers. |

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Why do my numbers change when I re-run a report by service date but stay the same by date posted?">
  Reports filtered by **Date Posted** are static: the activity that posted within a closed period never changes, so re-running the report months later returns identical numbers. Service-date reporting, by contrast, re-buckets activity as new payments and adjustments post against old dates of service, so historical balances can shift. For a reproducible, auditable close, run your reconciliation and roll-forward reports by **Date Posted**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why doesn't matched_posted_usd tie to insurance Paid in Encounter Revenue Activity?">
  `matched_posted_usd` covers only posted cash that is matched to a deposit. Add `unmatched_posted_usd` and `provider_level_adjustment_posted_usd`, then subtract the non-encounter provider adjustments (Payer Aux Cash, Payer Credit Account, and Payer Credits Untied) from the Provider Adjustments Report. See [Tie posted insurance to Encounter Revenue Activity](#tie-posted-insurance-to-encounter-revenue-activity).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A remittance is matched to a deposit but shows as unposted. Why isn't it on the ledger?">
  It was automatically routed to **Manual Review**, a guardrail that keeps the ledger from going out of balance. Common causes include conflicting patient-responsibility decisions, a remittance that doesn't balance to the ERA charges, a future check date, or an `OA23` denial from the primary payer. Resolve it in the **Posting Tool** (post or archive) as described in [Matched but unposted: get a remittance posted](#matched-but-unposted-get-a-remittance-posted).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What do I do if a deposit is in my bank but I can't find it in Insights?">
  The deposit likely hasn't been ingested yet, which is common with lockbox paper checks or ACH deposits not yet in the BAI2 feed. Contact your Athelas team to ingest it manually, and provide the bank account name, deposit date, amount, and bank reference or trace number. **Don't hold the month open.** Record the cash to Unposted Money and clear the match next cycle.
</Accordion>
