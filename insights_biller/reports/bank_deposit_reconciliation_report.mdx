The Bank Deposit Reconciliation Report accounts for every dollar that hit your bank. It breaks each bank deposit down to the checks matched to it and the claims paid on each check, and shows how much of that cash has been posted. This is the report you book cash from at month-end and reconcile to your bank statement.

It answers one question: *"For each dollar that landed in our bank account, do we know which check it came from, which claim it pays, and whether it has been posted yet?"*

**Where to find it:** **My Reports**, under the **Month End** tab or the **Revenue** tab, depending on how your site is set up.

## How Insights tracks your cash

Cash moves through a pipeline from the moment it lands in your bank account to the moment it is applied to a specific claim. At each stage, money is either fully matched or still in flight:

![Cash pipeline from payer payment to bank deposit, check, remittance, posted payment and encounter, with the unmatched, unlinked and unposted branches](/images/insights_biller/reports/bank_deposit_reconciliation_report/bank_deposit_reconciliation_report_1.png)

Money at any intermediate stage is **Unposted Money**: it is in your bank but not yet applied to claims. That is the balance your Unposted Money account carries on the ledger; see [Understanding Unposted Money](/insights_biller/reports/month_end_unposted_money).

The report does not give each pipeline stage its own column:

- A deposit that is not matched to any check shows in **unmatched_usd**.
- A check that is matched to a deposit but not yet fully posted shows in **matched_unposted_usd**, whether or not its remittance (ERA) has been linked yet.

## How each deposit is broken down

Each deposit is represented by three kinds of rows:

- **One line per claim on each check.** It shows how much of that check was matched and posted for that specific claim.
- **A check-level summary line** (claim number blank). It carries the cash on the check that has not been posted yet, plus any non-Athelas cash on the check.
- **A deposit-level summary line** (check number and claim number both blank). It carries cash on the deposit that is not tied to any specific check, plus any non-insurance cash recorded on the deposit.

A claim line can be split further when one claim's payments on a check touch more than one encounter, procedure, payer position or site. The split never changes the dollar totals.

### The four buckets

Every dollar of a deposit lands in one of four buckets or in the provider-level adjustment columns described below:

- **Matched & Posted**: insurance cash on a check that is matched to the deposit *and* posted, broken out per claim.
- **Matched & Unposted**: insurance cash on a matched check that has not been posted yet. This bucket is reported once per check, on the check-level summary line: the check amount, less non-Athelas cash, less what has been posted against the check, and less provider-level adjustments on the check.
- **Unmatched**: cash in the deposit that is not tied to any specific check, shown once per deposit on the deposit-level summary line.
- **Others**: non-insurance cash, such as patient payments and refunds recorded on the deposit, or money on a check for claims submitted outside Athelas.

Provider-level adjustments on matched checks (interest, forward balances, recoupments and other payer adjustments that are not tied to a claim) have their own columns, so a deposit still ties when a payer withholds or adds money at the check level. See the [Provider Adjustments Report](/insights_biller/reports/provider_adjustments_report) for the full breakdown.

## Two ways to run the report

Pick the mode in **Select Date Type**:

| **Mode** | **What it answers** | **Use it for** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Bank Deposit Date** | "What cash hit my bank in this window?" Rows are the deposits dated inside the range, and `total_cash_usd` ties to your bank statement. | **Booking cash.** This is the default. |
| **Date Posted** | "What was posted in this window?" Rows are the payments posted inside the range, with the deposit attached when one has been matched. | Investigating posting lag and payments whose deposit has not been found yet, and tying posted insurance to the [Encounter Revenue Activity Report](/insights_biller/reports/encounter_revenue_activity_report). |

Use **Bank Deposit Date** for the cash columns (`matched_unposted_usd`, `unmatched_usd`, `others_usd`, `total_cash_usd`) and **Date Posted** for the posted columns.

## Booking cash at month-end

<Tip>
  ✨**Smart Tip:** Book cash by **Bank Deposit Date**, not by Date Posted. Run the report for the month by Bank Deposit Date, sum the `total_cash_usd` column, and book **DR Cash / CR Unposted Money**. Start and end dates are inclusive. Keep the deposit-level report as audit support for the summarized entry, and set the **As-Of Date** to your close date to lock each deposit's status (matched, unmatched, posted or unposted) as of that day.
</Tip>

For the full set of cash entries, see [Month-End Close: Cash-Basis Entries](/insights_biller/reports/month_end_cash_entries).

### If Athelas receives your lockbox

Athelas sees every insurance deposit directly, so this report is a complete picture of your insurance cash. You can book cash from this report and reconcile the total to your bank statement as a check.

### If Athelas does not have your lockbox

The data in Insights may be incomplete. Book cash from your **bank statements**, since they are the source of truth for what actually landed, and use this report as a reconciliation tool to make sure Athelas knows about every deposit that needs posting. Missing lockbox data is the most common cause of reconciling items at month-end.

### A note on match before post

If your site posts payments only after they are matched to a bank deposit, posted totals trail deposits slightly while matching is in progress. That lag is normal and shows up as unposted cash. If your site posts on remittance receipt, payments can post before the deposit is found; the difference between deposit date and posted date is what keeps your cash and revenue entries consistent.

## Tying posted insurance to revenue

Run the report by **Date Posted** to tie the insurance you posted in a month to your revenue reports. Use the same Date Posted range on all three reports:

1. On this report, add up `matched_posted_usd` + `unmatched_posted_usd` + `provider_level_adjustment_posted_usd`.
2. From that total, subtract the `Total Amount (USD)` of the [Provider Adjustments Report](/insights_biller/reports/provider_adjustments_report) rows whose **Account Type** is **Payer Aux Cash**, **Payer Credit Account** or **Payer Credits Untied**. Run that report with **Include Non-Encounter Provider Adjustments** checked.
3. The result is designed to equal **Paid** on the [Encounter Revenue Activity Report](/insights_biller/reports/encounter_revenue_activity_report) for payer index 1 to 3 (primary, secondary and tertiary insurance).

See [Month-End Close](/insights_biller/reports/month_end_close) for the full walkthrough.

## Filters

![Bank Deposit Reconciliation report dialog showing Select Date Type, Start Date, End Date, Include Excluded Bank Deposits and As-Of Date](/images/insights_biller/reports/bank_deposit_reconciliation_report/bank_deposit_reconciliation_report_2.webp)

- **Select Date Type**: **Bank Deposit Date** (default) or **Date Posted**. See [Two ways to run the report](#two-ways-to-run-the-report).
- **Start Date** and **End Date**: the reporting window, inclusive.
- **Check Start Date** and **Check End Date** (Date Posted mode only): unposted provider adjustments have no posted date, so in Date Posted mode they are filtered by the date of the check they arrived on. Both default to the main date range.
- **Include Excluded Bank Deposits**: also include deposits that were excluded from reconciliation, and add a `deposit_inclusion_status` column that marks each deposit **INCLUDED** or **EXCLUDED**.
- **As-Of Date**: reports each deposit and check in the state Insights had recorded at the end of this day, so a rerun reproduces a prior close. The as-of date defaults to the end date. On a scheduled report, the as-of date is set to the date the report is generated.
- **Excel** or **CSV** output.

## Data dictionary

| **Column** | **Description** |
| :-- | :-- |
| **site**, **state** | The site name and state. Only present when the report is run at a parent site that has child sites. |
| **bank_deposit_id** | The ID of the bank deposit this row belongs to. Blank on Date Posted rows whose check is not on any included deposit. |
| **bank_deposit_link** | A direct link to this deposit in Insights, so you can open it and see the underlying detail. |
| **bank_deposit_date** | The date the deposit landed in the bank. |
| **deposit_inclusion_status** | **INCLUDED** or **EXCLUDED**. Only present when **Include Excluded Bank Deposits** is checked. |
| **check_number** | The check number of the check inside the deposit. Blank on deposit-level summary lines. |
| **payment_claim_submission_id** | The claim submission this row's posted cash pays. Blank on check-level and deposit-level summary lines. |
| **encounter_id** | The encounter the posted payment belongs to. Blank on summary lines. |
| **rendering_provider_id** | The rendering provider on that encounter. |
| **procedure_id** | The procedure the posted payment belongs to, or 0 when the row has no procedure (summary lines and claim-level adjustments). |
| **payer_index** | The payer position of the posted payment: 1 primary, 2 secondary, 3 tertiary. |
| **site_id** | The site the posted payment belongs to. On a parent-site run, this can be a child site. |
| **facility_name** | The facility on the claim. Only populated on claim lines. |
| **date_posted** | The earliest posted date of the payments for this claim on this check. |
| **notes** | Notes recorded on the bank deposit when it was reviewed. |
| **deposit_description** | The original transaction description from the bank feed or virtual card transaction the deposit came from. |
| **bank_account_last_4** | The last 4 digits of the bank account that received the deposit. Blank for virtual card transactions. |
| **payment_type** | The payment method codes on the check's remittances (for example ACH or CHK). |
| **matched_posted_usd** | Cash on this check and claim that is matched to the deposit *and* posted. |
| **matched_unposted_usd** | Insurance cash on a matched check that has not been posted: the check amount less non-Athelas cash, posted payments and provider-level adjustments on the check. Reported on the check-level summary line only. |
| **unmatched_usd** | Cash in the deposit that is not tied to any check. Reported once per deposit on the deposit-level summary line. |
| **others_usd** | Non-insurance cash: patient payments and refunds recorded on the deposit (deposit-level line), or money for claims submitted outside Athelas (check-level line). |
| **provider_level_adjustment_matched_posted_usd** | *Bank Deposit Date mode.* Posted provider-level adjustments on the deposit's matched checks. |
| **provider_level_adjustment_matched_unposted_usd** | *Bank Deposit Date mode.* Provider-level adjustments on the deposit's matched checks that have not been posted yet. Reported on the check-level summary line. |
| **provider_level_adjustment_posted_usd** | *Date Posted mode.* Provider-level adjustments posted in the date range, on matched and unmatched checks alike. |
| **provider_level_adjustment_unposted_usd** | *Date Posted mode.* Unposted provider-level adjustments on checks dated inside the check date range. |
| **unmatched_posted_usd** | *Date Posted mode.* Payments posted in the date range whose check is not on any included deposit yet. These rows have no deposit columns. |
| **total_cash_usd** | The cash this row accounts for. In Bank Deposit Date mode, the total across all of a deposit's rows equals the deposit's bank amount. |

In **Bank Deposit Date** mode, every row satisfies:

`total_cash_usd = matched_posted_usd + matched_unposted_usd + unmatched_usd + others_usd + provider_level_adjustment_matched_posted_usd + provider_level_adjustment_matched_unposted_usd`

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Which mode should I use to book cash for the month?">
  **Bank Deposit Date.** Its `total_cash_usd` ties to your bank statement. Date Posted mode is keyed to when payments were posted, so Date Posted is the mode for tying posted insurance to revenue, not for booking cash.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does my posted total trail my deposits?">
  Cash that has landed but has not been posted yet sits in `matched_unposted_usd` (matched to a check) or `unmatched_usd` (not matched to any check). That is your Unposted Money. If your site posts only after matching, some lag at month-end is normal. See [Understanding Unposted Money](/insights_biller/reports/month_end_unposted_money).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why do I get different numbers when I rerun last month's report?">
  Matching and posting continue after month-end, so a deposit that was unmatched at close may be matched and posted today. Set the **As-Of Date** to your original close date to reproduce the status each deposit had then.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We don't send our lockbox to Athelas. Can I still book cash from this report?">
  Book cash from your bank statements instead, and use this report to confirm that Athelas knows about every deposit that needs posting. Deposits Athelas has not seen are the most common reconciling item.
</Accordion>
