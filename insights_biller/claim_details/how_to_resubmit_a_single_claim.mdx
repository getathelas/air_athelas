### At a Glance

There are dozens of reasons a claim may be rejected or denied and need to be resubmitted — an incorrect CPT code, new insurance information, a typo anywhere. This guide shows how to fix a claim and resubmit it so your practice gets paid.

<Tip>
✨**Smart Tip:** Make your changes on the **claim** whenever you can, then **Save & Resubmit**. Editing the claim keeps everything tracked and posts correctly. Editing the submission directly bypasses billing rules and should be reserved for working around a misbehaving rule.
</Tip>

### Open the Claim You Want to Fix

You can start a resubmission from anywhere a claim appears — but the **Claims Page** is the most direct path. Go to the [Claims Page](https://insights.athelas.com) and find the claim you want to edit.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim_1.png"
  alt="The Claims Page with a list of claims"
  title="Claims Page"
/>

**Use the filters** to narrow the list quickly — you can search by anything from claim status to the patient's name to the submission ID.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim_2.png"
  alt="Using filters to find a specific claim to resubmit"
  title="Filter to find a claim"
/>

Open the claim, click the **Actions** menu, and select **Edit Claim Form & Resubmit**.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim_3.png"
  alt="Selecting Edit Claim Form and Resubmit from the Actions menu"
  title="Actions → Edit Claim Form & Resubmit"
/>

From here you can edit a large amount of information on the claim. The sections below follow the order they appear on your screen.

### Regenerate, or Save & Resubmit

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim_4.png"
  alt="The Regenerate and Save and Resubmit Claim buttons at the top of the editor"
  title="Regenerate / Save & Resubmit"
/>

At the top of the editing window, **Regenerate** resets the claim to its original state at the time of submission — useful if changes were made incorrectly or some piece of information has gone missing. **Save & Resubmit Claim** is where you'll go once you've made all your changes.

### Patient Information

The patient's information is pre-filled — correct any typos as needed.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim_7.png"
  alt="Editing patient and policy holder information on the claim"
  title="Patient Information"
/>

To update policy holder information, choose the patient's relationship to the policy holder at the top, then complete the required fields in the section below.

### Supporting Documentation

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim_8.png"
  alt="The Supporting Documentation section set to Not Required"
  title="Supporting Documentation"
/>

Insights defaults to **Not Required**. If documentation is required, choose **Required** and attach the files. Once you pick a cover letter type, it's auto-generated — a **Preview** button appears next to the Cover Letter menu so you can review it.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim_9.png"
  alt="Previewing an auto-generated cover letter for supporting documentation"
  title="Preview the cover letter"
/>

By default, cover letters and attachments are sent automatically with the resubmission. To send the documents on their own, click **Manually Send Now**. You can also **Preview CMS-1500 Form** to see the form that goes out with the documentation.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim_10.png"
  alt="The Manually Send Now option for sending documentation on its own"
  title="Manually Send Now"
/>

<Note>
Sending supporting documentation can be done **separately** from resubmitting a whole claim. Go straight to this section, attach the documents, and click **Manually Send Now** — only the documents are sent. For a dedicated walkthrough, see [How to Send Documentation](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_send_documentation).
</Note>

### Diagnosis Codes and Service Lines

To add a diagnosis code, click **+ Diagnosis Code** and enter it; remove one with the trash icon.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim_11.png"

  title="Diagnosis codes"

Service lines work the same way. Edit an existing line with its pencil icon, or click **+ Service Line**, fill out the popup, and click **Create Service Line** to add a new one.

### All Other Sections

The remaining sections are straightforward data entry — and prime candidates for typos. Double-check each for human error:

- Claim Information
- Billing Information
- Rendering Information
- Referring Information
- Supervising Information
- Service Facility Information
- Other Information

<Warning>
**Resubmitting to a non-Medicare/Medicaid payer as a corrected claim?** Do both of the following before you resubmit:

- Set the **Claim Frequency Code** to **Corrected Claim (7)** in the Claim Information section.
- Enter the **Previous Claim Control Number** in the Other Information section (if it isn't already filled in).
</Warning>

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim_13.png"
  alt="Setting the Claim Frequency Code to Corrected Claim for a corrected resubmission"
  title="Corrected claim settings"
/>

When everything looks right, click **Save & Resubmit Claim**.

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Should I edit the claim or the submission?">
Edit the **claim** whenever possible, then **Save & Resubmit**. Changes to the claim run through billing rules, stay tracked, and post correctly. Editing the submission directly bypasses billing rules — reserve it for working around a specific rule when you truly need to.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does Regenerate do?">
**Regenerate** resets the claim to its original state at the time of submission. It's useful when edits were made incorrectly or a piece of information has gone missing and you want a clean starting point before making changes again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to resubmit the whole claim just to send a document?">
No. Use the **Supporting Documentation** section (or **Actions → Send Documentation**) and click **Manually Send Now** to send documents on their own without resubmitting the claim. See [How to Send Documentation](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_send_documentation).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I resubmit many claims at once?">
Use the bulk workflow instead — group your worklist, select the claims, and resubmit them together. See [How to Resubmit Claims in Bulk](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_claims_in_bulk).
</Accordion>
