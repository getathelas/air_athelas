### At a Glance

When the same problem affects many claims — an eligibility denial across a patient's visits, a batch of submission errors with the same cause — you don't have to fix them one at a time. The Claims Page lets you build a focused worklist, select a group of claims, and resubmit them all in a single action.

<Tip>
✨**Smart Tip:** Bulk actions turn what used to be many clicks into a few. An eligibility denial on five claims for one patient — update insurance, resubmit, and comment — drops from roughly 15 actions to about 3.
</Tip>

### Step 1 — Build a Focused Worklist

Bulk resubmission works best when you first gather the claims that share a problem. Filter your list and **group by Status Reason** so claims with the same denial, rejection, or submission-error reason land in one bucket. For the full set of tools, see [Organizing Your Claims Worklist](/insights_biller/claim_details/organizing_your_claims_worklist).

### Step 2 — Select the Claims

Select the claims you want to resubmit using the checkboxes. To grab an entire bucket, **right-click the group's header row** and choose **Select all in group**.

<Note>
The list loads 50 claims at a time as you scroll, and selecting all captures only the claims that are loaded. To work a bigger bucket, scroll down to load more rows first, then select again. Most bulk actions work on up to **500 claims** at once.
</Note>

### Step 3 — Resubmit with More Actions

With your claims selected, open **More Actions** at the bottom of the screen and choose **Submit Claims**. The action submits every selected claim at once, including claims that were submitted before.

![The More Actions list for 50 selected claims](/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_claims_in_bulk/how_to_resubmit_claims_in_bulk_1.webp)

From the same **More Actions** menu you can run other bulk operations on the selected claims — **Update Primary Insurance**, **Comment on Claims**, **Push to PR**, **Mark as Intended to Bill**, or **Defer**. For the full list, see [Working with Many Claims at Once](/insights_biller/claim_details/claim_details_page#working-with-many-claims-at-once).

<Warning>
Higher-risk actions like **Push to PR**, **Push to Next Payer**, and **Adjust** show you what will happen before they run, so you can review the change first.
</Warning>

<Tip>
✨**Smart Tip:** For a niche change across a group — say, "add a KX modifier to all 99214s on these claims" — click **Ask Athelas AI** in the bar at the bottom of the screen and describe the edit. [Athelas Assistant](/insights_general/athelas_assistant/getting_started_with_athelas_assistant) works with your selection as context.
</Tip>

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Where do I find bulk resubmission?">
It's built into the Claims Page. Select claims from your worklist, then open **More Actions** at the bottom of the screen and choose **Submit Claims**. There's no separate bulk-resubmission page to visit.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many claims can I resubmit at once?">
Most bulk actions work on up to **500 claims** at once. The list loads 50 claims at a time, and selecting all grabs everything currently loaded, so scroll to the bottom of a group before you select the whole bucket.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I bulk-update insurance before resubmitting?">
Yes. **Update Primary Insurance** is one of the bulk actions in the **More Actions** menu — useful when an eligibility denial hits several of one patient's claims. The action works only on claims from a single patient, so select that patient's claims, update the insurance, then resubmit them together.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if I only need to fix one claim?">
Open the claim from the [Claims Page](/insights_biller/claim_details/claim_details_page#submitting-claims) and click **Preview Submission**. Correct the claim (or use the pencil icon to edit the submission), then click **Resubmit**.
</Accordion>
