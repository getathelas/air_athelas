### At a Glance

This walkthrough shows how to upload documentation that isn't sent automatically by one of your organization's rules. (Your organization can set up rules to attach supporting documentation to claims automatically.) You can send documents on their own, or as part of resubmitting the whole claim.

### Option 1 — Send Supporting Documentation Only

On the [Claims Page](https://insights.athelas.com), open the claim that needs documentation. Click the **Actions** menu and choose **Send Documentation**.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_send_documentation/how_to_send_documentation_1.png"
  alt="Selecting Send Documentation from the Actions menu on a claim"
  title="Actions → Send Documentation"
/>

Fill out the form in the popup, upload the documentation, and click **Send Documentation**.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_send_documentation/how_to_send_documentation_2.png"
  alt="The Send Documentation popup with a form and file upload"
  title="Send Documentation popup"
/>

### Option 2 — Modify and Resubmit the Whole Claim

From the [Claims Page](https://insights.athelas.com), open a claim, click the **Actions** menu, and choose **Edit Claim Form & Resubmit**.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_send_documentation/how_to_send_documentation_3.png"
  alt="Selecting Edit Claim Form and Resubmit from the Actions menu"
  title="Actions → Edit Claim Form & Resubmit"
/>

A popup opens with editable fields for the whole claim — scroll down, since it's a long form. Attach your documentation in the **Supporting Documentation** section, update any other fields, then click **Save & Resubmit Claim** at the top of the form.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_send_documentation/how_to_send_documentation_4.png"
  alt="The Save and Resubmit Claim button at the top of the claim edit form"
  title="Save & Resubmit Claim"
/>

For the full resubmission walkthrough, see [How to Resubmit a Single Claim](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim).

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Do I have to resubmit the claim to send a document?">
No. Use **Actions → Send Documentation** to send documents on their own, without resubmitting. Only use **Edit Claim Form & Resubmit** when you also need to change the claim itself.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can documentation be attached automatically?">
Yes. Your organization can create rules that automatically attach and send supporting documentation with claims. This manual process is for documents that fall outside those rules, or one-off attachments.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where does the cover letter come from?">
When documentation is required on a claim, Insights auto-generates a cover letter based on the type you select. You can preview it before it goes out — see the Supporting Documentation section in [How to Resubmit a Single Claim](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_a_single_claim).
</Accordion>
