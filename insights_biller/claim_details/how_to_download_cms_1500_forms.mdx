### At a Glance

This guide shows how to download and print claim forms — individually or in bulk — from the Claims Page. Insights generates the right form automatically based on the payer: **CMS-1500** for professional claims and **UB-04** for institutional claims.

**What is a CMS-1500 form?** The CMS-1500 (formerly HCFA-1500) is the standard claim form used to bill professional services to Medicare, Medicaid, Tricare, and commercial payers. For more, visit [cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/billing/electronicbillingeditrans/1500).

### Download an Individual Form

On the [Claims Page](https://insights.athelas.com), open the target claim.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_download_cms_1500_forms/how_to_download_cms_1500_forms_1.png"
  alt="Opening a claim and its Submissions section on the Claims Page"
  title="Open the claim"
/>

Open the **Submissions** section, find the submission you want, and click **View** in the Action column. A preview of the form opens (it may take 10–15 seconds to load). Use the icon buttons at the top of the preview to **Download** or **Print** the form.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_download_cms_1500_forms/how_to_download_cms_1500_forms_2.png"
  alt="Previewing a CMS-1500 form with Download and Print buttons"
  title="Download or print the form"
/>

<Tip>
✨**Smart Tip:** Printing on pre-printed red claim paper? In the form preview, **remove the background** so only the data prints — this lines the fields up on RED-certified CMS-1500 paper. No feature flag is required.
</Tip>

### Download Forms in Bulk

Use the **Download CMS Forms** button next to the filters at the top of the Claims Page.

<Warning>
Narrow your list with filters first. Downloading forms for an unfiltered list can take a very long time or fail.
</Warning>

In this example, forms are downloaded for a single patient, limited to claims with an **Approved** status.

<img
  src="/images/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_download_cms_1500_forms/how_to_download_cms_1500_forms_3.png"

Large bulk downloads may take a few minutes to prepare.

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Will I get a CMS-1500 or a UB-04?">
Insights picks the form automatically from the payer and claim type — **CMS-1500** for professional claims, **UB-04** for institutional claims. The submission preview shows the form type in its top corner so you can confirm at a glance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I print on pre-printed red claim paper?">
In the form preview, remove the background so only the field data prints. That aligns the data with the boxes on RED-certified CMS-1500 paper. Then print as usual.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My bulk download is taking forever — what happened?">
The list was likely too broad. Apply filters (patient, status, date range) to narrow it before clicking **Download CMS Forms**. Even a well-filtered bulk download can take a few minutes to prepare.
</Accordion>
