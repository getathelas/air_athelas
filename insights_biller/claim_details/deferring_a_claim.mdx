### At a Glance

Sometimes a claim has work to be done — but not right now. Maybe you're waiting on a callback from a payer, an appeal in litigation, or a coding update from a provider. **Deferring** lets you set a claim aside so it drops out of your worklist until it's actionable again, without losing track of it.

Deferring solves three everyday problems:

- Clearing claims out of your view when there's nothing you can do at the moment.
- Capturing *why* and *how often* claims get set aside, so your team can spot patterns.
- Making it clear when you can (or can't) expect an update from your EHR.

<Note>
Deferrals work only on the new Claims Page. They aren't compatible with the legacy denials or rejections worklists.
</Note>

## What Happens When You Defer a Claim

Deferring **hides** a claim from views that filter for `Claim is not deferred` — most importantly the **My Claims** and **Workable Claims** views, which exclude deferred claims automatically.

When the expiration date arrives, one of two things happens:

- If the claim's status has **changed** in the meantime (because new data moved the claim forward), no further work is needed — the deferral did its job.
- If the status is **unchanged**, the claim reappears in your worklist so you can pick it back up.

<Warning>
Deferring **does not change the claim's status** — it only sets the claim aside in its current status. The date, reason, and duration of every deferral are recorded in the [Activity Feed](/insights_biller/claim_details/working_a_claim).
</Warning>

## How to Defer a Claim

Open the claim's **Actions** menu and choose **Mark as Deferred**.

![Mark as Deferred in a claim's Actions menu](/images/insights_biller/claim_details/deferring_a_claim/deferring_a_claim_1.webp)

In the **Mark as Deferred** dialog, choose a **Reason** and an **Expiration Date**. Both are required. Pick **Tomorrow**, **1 Week**, **1 Month**, or **2 Months**, or click **+** to choose a custom date up to 90 days out.

![The Mark as Deferred dialog with reasons and expiration dates](/images/insights_biller/claim_details/deferring_a_claim/deferring_a_claim_2.webp)

Click **Defer**, and the claim disappears from your workable views until the deferral expires or the claim's status changes.

<Tip>
✨**Smart Tip:** To check whether a claim is currently deferred, open its **Actions** menu — the options shown tell you its deferral state.
</Tip>

## Deferring in Bulk

You can defer — and cancel deferrals — for many claims at once. Select the claims, open **More Actions** at the bottom of the screen, and choose **Defer** (or **Cancel Deferral**). The **Defer Claims** dialog works the same way as for a single claim.

![Defer and Cancel Deferral in the More Actions list](/images/insights_biller/claim_details/deferring_a_claim/deferring_a_claim_3.webp)

One thing to know:

- If you defer a claim that's **already deferred**, the expiration date **resets** to the new date — it does not add on to the existing deferral.

## Canceling or Extending a Deferral

Once a claim is deferred, you can **cancel** the deferral at any time: open the claim's **Actions** menu and choose **Cancel Deferral**, or use **Cancel Deferral** in **More Actions** for several claims.

To **extend** a deferral, re-defer the claim with a new expiration date. Re-deferring without first canceling is supported through the bulk action.

## Creating Deferral Reasons

To add a new reason, click the **+** button next to the reasons, type it under **New deferral reason**, and click **Add Reason**.

![Adding a new deferral reason](/images/insights_biller/claim_details/deferring_a_claim/deferring_a_claim_4.webp)

<Note>
Deferral reasons are **site-specific** — once anyone at your site creates a reason, everyone at that site sees it as an option. Reasons can't be deleted once created (the same behavior as claim tags), so agree on a shared set of reasons before you go wide.
</Note>

## Automatic Behavior

Deferrals take care of a few things on their own:

- **Auto-clear** — a deferral clears automatically when it **expires** or when the claim's **status changes**. Both events are logged in the Activity Feed.
- **Pauses patient responsibility** — while a claim is deferred, [patient responsibility (PR)](/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/patient_responsibility_page) generation is paused, so you won't send PR to a patient while you're still waiting on the claim.

## The "Updated in EHR" Reason (Billing-Only Sites)

Sites that use Insights for billing with an external EHR (not the Air EHR) get a built-in reason: **Updated in EHR**. Use it after you fix a claim in your EHR. When you choose it, the expiration date is set to **Next EHR Import**, the next time Insights imports that claim from your EHR; hover the option to see the date. You can still pick a different date.

![The Updated in EHR reason with Next EHR Import selected](/images/insights_biller/claim_details/deferring_a_claim/deferring_a_claim_5.png)

- Used in bulk, each claim is set to its own next import date.
- If the reason is grayed out, hover it to see why. It is unavailable when EHR import isn't turned on for your site, when a claim has no EHR Claim ID, or when its date of service is too long ago to import again — a guardrail so you can't wait on an update that will never arrive.

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Does deferring change a claim's status?">
No. Deferring only sets the claim aside in its current status and hides it from your workable views. When the deferral expires — if the status hasn't advanced on its own — the claim reappears exactly where it was.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I see that a claim was deferred, and by whom?">
Every deferral is recorded in the claim's **Activity Feed** with who deferred it, the reason, and the duration. To check a claim's current deferral state, open its **Actions** menu.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I defer a claim that's already deferred?">
The expiration date **resets** to the new date you choose — it doesn't extend the original deferral. This applies whether you re-defer a single claim or a batch.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is the 'Updated in EHR' reason missing or grayed out?">
It only appears for billing-only sites (those not on the Air EHR). Even then, it's disabled for a claim that Insights can't import again — one with no EHR Claim ID, or a date of service too long ago — so you don't defer a claim that would never receive an update. Hover the grayed-out reason to see which applies.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I still send patient responsibility while a claim is deferred?">
No — PR generation is paused for the duration of the deferral. This prevents a patient statement from going out while you're still working the claim with the payer. PR resumes once the deferral clears.
</Accordion>
