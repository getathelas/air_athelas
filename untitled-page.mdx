These are the steps to set up Prescription Drug Monitoring within our system

- **Step 1: Navigate to Customer Connect**
  - Visit [https://connect.bamboohealth.com](https://connect.bamboohealth.com)
- **Step 2: Create an Account**
  - Click “Create an Account” (top right).
- **Step 3: Complete the Application**
  - Enter the required details and follow the guided steps.

  - Key notes:
    - Multi-state customers can sign up for all states at once.
    - On the Organization screen, users select all facility location states.
  - Customers may purchase services for non-funded states during this process
    - Unless a custom onboarding process prohibits this. Non Funded State list (as of Y2026):
      - **California (CA)** – non-funded (providers pay)
      - **Colorado (CO)** – non-funded (providers pay)
      - **Hawaii (HI)** – non-funded (providers pay)
      - **Kentucky (KY)** – non-funded (providers pay)
      - **Maryland (MD)** – non-funded (providers pay)
      - **Mississippi (MS)** – non-funded (providers pay)
      - **Pennsylvania (PA)** – non-funded (providers pay)
      - **Rhode Island (RI)** – non-funded (providers pay)
      - **South Dakota (SD)** – non-funded (providers pay)
      - **Texas (TX)** – _note:_ Texas historically was non-funded in some sources, but recent state-funded access may exist now — always confirm with Bamboo.
      - **Utah (UT)** – non-funded or limited (providers pay for PMP data)
      - **Washington (WA)** – non-funded (providers pay)
      - **Wyoming (WY)** – non-funded (providers pay)
  - Customer Connect dynamically shows available funding & products per state.
  - Required documents (e.g., PMP Gateway Licensee Profile, EULA) appear within the workflow.
  - Users may save progress and return later.
  - After completion, Bamboo Health sends a PDMP query request to the state PDMP administrator.
  - Accounts remain “pending” until state approval is complete.
- **Step 4: Credential Issuance**
  - Once approved (estimated 2–4 weeks):
  - Credentials are issued to the **EMR Integration Partner (Athelas Air)** or the designated health-entity contact.
  - Distribution depends on prior integration agreements.

Note: PDMP approval takes about 2-4 weeks, so it is important we get started on this soon.
