This guide covers the claims in Insights that need attention, how they differ, and where to work each one. They represent different stages in the claim lifecycle, from data entering the system to the payer's final decision.

Import errors are worked on their own page, under **Action Items** > **Import Errors**. Everything after that point — submission errors, rejections, and denials — is worked on the **Claims Page**. For a hands-on walkthrough of the Claims Page, see [**Getting Started as a Biller**](/insights_onboard/getting_started_as_a_biller) and [**Getting Started with the Claims Page**](/insights_biller/claim_details/claim_details_page).

<Note>
The separate **Submission Errors**, **Rejections**, and **Denials Worklist** pages under **Action Items** have been retired. The Claims Page replaces all three: filter by **Claim Status** to see the same claims in one list.
</Note>

## Claims Page

The **Claims Page** is the primary workspace for billers. It brings together encounter data, claim details, rule outcomes, submission history, and payer responses for every claim.

**When to use:**

- Working submission errors, rejections, and denials
- Reviewing claim-level details end to end
- Validating rule outcomes and submission history

### Find What You Need to Work On

The Claims Page shows every claim at your practice. With thousands of claims, the key is narrowing down to exactly what you need.

**Start from a built-in view.** Views appear as tabs across the top of the page. **Workable Claims** holds every claim that is your site's responsibility, needs work now, and isn't deferred. It is grouped by **Status Reason**, so each bucket holds claims with the same problem.

**Use filters to focus your work.** Click **Filter** (or press `F`) to narrow down your list. Common filters include:

- **Claim Status** — what needs to happen next on the claim, such as **Submission Errors**, **Rejections**, or **Full Denials**
- **Status Reason** — why the claim is in its status, such as a specific denial or rejection reason
- **Responsible Party** — your site or Athelas
- **Assignee** — claims assigned to you or to a teammate
- **Insurances** — the primary, secondary, or tertiary payer
- **Dates** — service, submission, created, or posted dates

Combine filters to get exactly what you need. For example: **Claim Status** is **Rejections** and **Responsible Party** is your site.

### Understanding Claim Statuses

Three fields describe where a claim is and what it needs:

- **Status** — what needs to happen next on the claim, and whether it needs action.
- **Stage** — which party holds the balance right now: a payer or the patient.
- **Payer status** — where the claim stands with one specific payer.

For every value these three fields can take, and which statuses you can act on, see [**Claim Status, Stage & Payer Status**](/insights_biller/claim_details/status_and_stages).

## Open Encounters

An **open encounter** is a claim that has been stopped for site review before going through the submission funnel. It remains in an "open" status until you review it; after review, it can be submitted or corrected.

**How to view Open Encounters:**

1. Log in to Insights.
2. Navigate to the **Claims** page.
3. Open **Filter** and set:
   - **Working Status:** Open
   - **Service Date Range:** Your go-live or backfill date (as needed)
4. Scroll to the **Tags** column to see which rule stopped this encounter from closing.
5. Click into the encounter, review based on the tag, and make any changes.
6. Click **Submit** at the top right when ready.

![The Working Status filter options: Open, Closed, In Progress, Blocked, and Not Intended To Bill](/images/insights_onboard/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials_4.webp)

<Tip>
You can save these filters as a view: set your filters, then click **Save** next to the filter bar.
</Tip>

## Claims That Need Attention: The Four Types

Claims that need attention fall into four types, depending on **where** in the lifecycle the issue occurred.

| **Type** | **Origin** | **Has Claim Been Submitted?** | **Where You Work It** |
| :------- | :--------- | :---------------------------- | :-------------------- |
| **Import Errors** | EHR → Insights | No | **Action Items** > **Import Errors** |
| **Submission Errors** | Insights rules engine or clearinghouse checks | No | Claims Page, **Claim Status** is **Submission Errors** |
| **Rejections** | Clearinghouse / payer gateway | Yes (not adjudicated) | Claims Page, **Claim Status** is **Rejections** |
| **Denials** | Payer | Yes | Claims Page, **Claim Status** is **Full Denials** or **Partial Denials** |

The last three types are claim **Statuses**, not **Stages** — a claim's stage names the payer or the patient who holds the balance. See [**Claim Status, Stage & Payer Status**](/insights_biller/claim_details/status_and_stages) for both value lists.

### Who Owns the Claim: Site or Athelas

Every claim is either your **site's responsibility** or **Athelas' responsibility**. Most submission errors, rejections, and denials start with Athelas, and the Athelas team works to resolve and resubmit them.

When your team must provide information or take action, the claim moves to your site's responsibility. Claims your site owns that need work now appear in the **Workable Claims** view. Check it daily: timely work on these claims is critical for claim turnaround.

- To see one side only, filter **Responsible Party** to your site or to **Athelas**.
- A claim that is Athelas' responsibility shows the **Athelas** logo as its assignee. There's nothing for you to do on it.
- To hand a claim to Athelas, open the **Assignee** picker (or press `A` on an open claim) and choose **Athelas**. You'll be asked to give a reason, which is logged to the claim's Activity Feed.

<Accordion title="Coming from the old worklists? Here is where each bucket went">
| **Old bucket or status** | **On the Claims Page** |
| :----------------------- | :--------------------- |
| **More Info Required** / **Site Action Required** | **Responsible Party** is your site. These claims appear in **Workable Claims**. |
| **Athelas Responsibility** | **Responsible Party** is **Athelas**. |
| **Not Started** | Claims in **Workable Claims** that no one has worked yet. Assign them to track who picks each one up. |
| **Updated in EHR** / **Awaiting EHR Updates** | After you fix the claim in your EHR, defer it with the **Updated in EHR** reason. It drops out of your worklist until the next EHR import. See [Deferring a Claim](/insights_biller/claim_details/deferring_a_claim). |
| **Blocked** | Assign the claim to **Athelas** and explain what you need in the reason. |
| **Not Workable** / **Written Off** | Use **Adjust** from the claim's **Actions** menu to write off the balance. |
</Accordion>

## Import Errors

**Definition:** An **Import Error** occurs when encounter or claim-related data fails to fully move from the EHR into Insights. These errors happen before a claim is created or sent to the clearinghouse.

**How to view Import Errors:**

1. Log in to Insights.
2. Navigate to **Action Items** > **Import Errors**.

### Import Errors: Common Causes

Import errors typically happen when:

- **Missing key details** — A patient or encounter is missing essential information.
- **New entities not yet added** — A new provider was added in your EHR but not yet in Insights.
- **Missing insurance information** — A patient is missing their insurance member ID.
- **Unrecognized codes** — An encounter uses a custom CPT code that Insights does not recognize.
- **Potential duplicates** — A patient appears to be a duplicate, and Insights pauses import for investigation.

### How Import Errors Work

- **Detection** — When data cannot be properly imported, it is flagged as an import error.
- **Categorization** — Errors are grouped by type (e.g., "Invalid place of service code").
- **Prioritization** — The dashboard shows errors by total value and number of affected claims.
- **Resolution** — Some errors are resolved by the Athelas Ops team; others require action from your team.

### Resolving Import Errors

You can address import errors by:

- **Updating your EHR** — Fix missing or incorrect data in your EHR so it can flow into Insights.
- **Using the Import feature** — Click the blue **Import** button to open the encounter creation flow and correct data directly in Insights.
- **Working with Athelas** — Contact your Athelas representative for new providers/facilities or to create import rules.

<Tip>
Review import errors weekly to catch issues early. Notify Athelas when adding new providers or facilities. Most errors are simple missing fields that can be fixed in your EHR; the corrected data will flow into Insights during the next overnight import.
</Tip>

**Note:** The goal is to work toward zero outstanding import errors so all eligible encounters are captured for claim submission.

## Submission Errors

**Definition:** A **Submission Error** occurs when a claim is blocked before it reaches the payer. It failed a pre-submission check or billing rule, clearinghouse verification, or transmission.

Common causes include:

- Missing modifiers on procedure codes
- Incorrect diagnosis codes (ICD-10)
- Invalid addresses
- Clearinghouse rule violations
- Incorrect CPT codes
- Typos in patient, billing, or provider information
- Missing required fields (e.g., DOB, insurance ID, provider NPI)
- Invalid characters in the claim data
- Incorrect insurance information

These claims are **pre-submission** and fully controllable within Insights.

**How to view Submission Errors:**

1. Log in to Insights and open the **Claims** page.
2. Open **Workable Claims**, or click **Filter** and set **Claim Status** to **Submission Errors**.
3. Group by **Status Reason** so each bucket holds claims blocked by the same error.

![The Claims Page filtered to Claim Status Submission Errors](/images/insights_onboard/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials_5.webp)

### How to Resolve Submission Errors

1. Click a claim to open it. The **Status Reason** section of **Claim Context** lists the errors blocking the claim.
2. Fix the claim in one of these ways:
   - **Fix it in Insights.** Edit the claim, then click **Submit** (**Resubmit** on a claim that was submitted before). To check the fix first, choose **Review** from the **Actions** menu to dry-run the claim through billing rules.
   - **Fix it in your EHR.** Correct the data in your EHR, then open **Actions** > **Mark as Deferred** and choose the **Updated in EHR** reason. The claim waits for the next EHR import.
   - **Hand it to Athelas.** If you can't fix it, assign the claim to **Athelas** and say what you need in the reason.

![A claim with a submission error in Status Reason and the Actions menu open](/images/insights_onboard/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials_6.webp)

When many claims share the same error, fix and submit them together. See [**How to Resubmit Claims in Bulk**](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_claims_in_bulk).

**Note:** Claims may move back and forth between your site and Athelas if more information is needed after a claim was handed off.

## Rejections

**Definition:** A **Rejection** occurs when a claim is returned by the clearinghouse or payer gateway due to formatting, compliance, or eligibility issues. The claim is never accepted for adjudication. These are not payer decisions—they are technical or validation failures after the claim was submitted.

<Warning>
A rejected claim is effectively invisible to the insurance company because it was never accepted into their system. This creates significant risk for timely filing. Rejections should be reviewed and resolved promptly.
</Warning>

**How to view Rejections:**

1. Log in to Insights and open the **Claims** page.
2. Open **Workable Claims**, or click **Filter** and set **Claim Status** to **Rejections**.
3. Group by **Status Reason** so claims with the same rejection reason land together.

![The Claims Page filtered to Claim Status Rejections](/images/insights_onboard/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials_7.webp)

### How to Resolve Rejections

1. Click a claim to open it. The **Status Reason** section of **Claim Context** shows the clearinghouse's rejection reason, and **Submissions** shows each submission sent.

   ![A rejected claim's Status Reason with the clearinghouse's rejection reason](/images/insights_onboard/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials_8.webp)

2. Fix the claim in one of these ways:
   - **Fix it in Insights.** Edit the claim, then click **Resubmit**.
   - **Fix it in your EHR.** Correct the data in your EHR, then defer the claim with the **Updated in EHR** reason so Athelas can re-extract and resubmit it after the next import.
   - **Hand it to Athelas.** If you can't fix the rejection, assign the claim to **Athelas** with a reason.

For step-by-step resubmission, see [**Getting Started with the Claims Page**](/insights_biller/claim_details/claim_details_page#submitting-claims) for a single claim, or [**How to Resubmit Claims in Bulk**](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_claims_in_bulk) for many at once.

## Denials

**Definition:** A **Denial** is a formal decision from the payer after the claim has been received and adjudicated. The payer has reviewed the claim and decided that payment will not be made in full or in part. Denials have two statuses:

- **Full Denials** — the payer allowed nothing on the claim.
- **Partial Denials** — the payer paid some lines and denied others.

For analytics and trends, see the [**Denials Analysis**](/insights_biller/analytics/the_denials_analysis_page) page.

**How to view Denials:**

1. Log in to Insights and open the **Claims** page.
2. Open **Workable Claims**, or click **Filter** and set **Claim Status** to **Full Denials** and **Partial Denials**.
3. Group by **Status Reason** to work denials by cause. To focus on specific denial codes, filter by **CARC** or **RARC**.

![The Claims Page filtered to Full Denials and Partial Denials](/images/insights_onboard/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials_9.webp)

### How to Resolve Denials

1. Click a claim to open it. For a denial, the **Status Reason** section of **Claim Context** shows the CARC and RARC codes with plain-language descriptions, the denied amount, and the denied procedures.

   ![A denied claim's Status Reason with CARC and RARC codes and the affected procedures](/images/insights_onboard/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials/getting_started_as_biller_errors_rejections_and_denials_10.webp)

2. Decide what the denial needs:
   - **Correct and resubmit.** Fix the claim in Insights and click **Resubmit**, or fix it in your EHR and defer it with the **Updated in EHR** reason.
   - **Appeal or send records.** Open **Actions** > **Request** and choose **Request Appeal Submission** or **Request Medical Records Submission**. The claim moves to **Packet Pending** while the packet is prepared. See [**How to Send Documentation**](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_send_documentation).
   - **Move the balance on.** Use **Push to Next Payer** or **Push to PR** from the **Actions** menu when the balance belongs to another payer or the patient.
   - **Write it off.** When no action will lead to payment, use **Adjust** from the **Actions** menu to write off the balance.
   - **Hand it to Athelas.** If you're stuck, assign the claim to **Athelas** with a reason.

<Tip>
Check your denials daily. Start with older dates of service to avoid timely filing issues, and order groups by balance to work the largest revenue impact first. Save your setup as a view — for example, "Denials by reason" — so it's one click away.
</Tip>

### FAQ

<Accordion title="What's the difference between an error, a rejection, and a denial?">
**Import and Submission Errors** happen before a claim is successfully sent to the payer: import errors between your EHR and Insights, submission errors inside Insights (validation or blocking rules). **Rejections** happen after you submit—the clearinghouse or gateway returns the claim for technical/format/eligibility reasons, so the payer never adjudicates it. **Denials** happen after the payer has received and adjudicated the claim and decided not to pay (in full or in part).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where did the Submission Errors, Rejections, and Denials Worklist pages go?">
They have been retired. The Claims Page shows the same claims: filter **Claim Status** to **Submission Errors**, **Rejections**, **Full Denials**, or **Partial Denials**, or open **Workable Claims** to see all of them that your site owns in one list.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I see only the claims my team needs to work?">
Open **Workable Claims**. It shows claims that are your site's responsibility, are in a status that needs work, and aren't deferred. Claims that are Athelas' responsibility are left out, because the Athelas team is working them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I fixed a claim in my EHR. What do I do in Insights?">
Open the claim's **Actions** menu, choose **Mark as Deferred**, and pick the **Updated in EHR** reason. The claim leaves your worklist until the next EHR import brings in your fix. This reason is available on sites that bill through Insights with an external EHR. See [Deferring a Claim](/insights_biller/claim_details/deferring_a_claim).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I go to resubmit a denied or rejected claim?">
Open the claim on the Claims Page, make your corrections, and click **Resubmit**. For one claim, see [**Getting Started with the Claims Page**](/insights_biller/claim_details/claim_details_page#submitting-claims). For many at once, use [**How to Resubmit Claims in Bulk**](/insights_biller/claim_details/how_to_resubmit_claims_in_bulk).
</Accordion>
