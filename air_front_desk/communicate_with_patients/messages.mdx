Air maintains an internal and external messaging functionality that lets sites communicate with other site staff, and directly with patients through the Patient Portal.

## Messaging from Insights

Messages in Insights can be accessed by clicking the **Messages** tab under **Utilities**. From there, the **DM** tab lists all existing chats for the user. To create a new chat, click **+ New Chat** at the bottom of the pane.

![Messages tab with DM list and New Chat button](/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_1.webp)

To add recipients, type their names in the box containing **Start a new message**:

1. **External patients** are designated with `Patient: XXX`.
2. **Internal staff** show just their name.

![Patient recipient with Patient label](/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_2.webp)

![Internal staff recipient](/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_3.webp)

<Warning>
  Multiple recipients can be added to a message to create a group text. **Any recipients of a group text can see all other recipients — including external patients.** This is <u>not</u> a private text blast feature.
</Warning>

![Group text with multiple recipients](/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_4.webp)

From here, pressing **Enter** or clicking out of the recipient menu generates a new message window where you can type and send your message. Messages also support attaching files — click **+ Add An Attachment** at the bottom of the pane.

![New message window with attachment button](/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_5.webp)

![File attached in message](/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_6.webp)

## New messages

New messages appear at the top of the messages list with an unread indicator. Clicking the message shows the full thread, where you can respond.

![Unread message at top of list](/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_7.webp)

![Opened message with response field](/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_8.webp)

## Messaging from the Athelas Patient Portal

For patients, messages sent from Insights can be accessed in the Patient Portal. Patients are notified of new messages via text or email alerts.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <img src="/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_9.webp" alt="Text message notification to patient" title="SMS notification" />
  <img src="/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_10.webp" alt="Email notification to patient" title="Email notification" />
</Columns>

After logging into the portal, patients access messages via the **Messages** tab in the sidebar.

![Patient portal Messages tab](/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_11.webp)

From here, patients can see existing conversations and send replies. At this time, patients **cannot create new conversations** from the portal — all conversations must be initiated by the provider or site staff.

![Patient portal conversation view](/images/air_front_desk/communicate_with_patients/messages/messages_12.webp)

### FAQ

<Accordion title="Can patients start a new conversation from the portal?">
  No. All conversations must be initiated from Insights by provider or site staff. Once a thread exists, the patient can reply freely from the portal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How are patients notified of a new message?">
  Patients receive either an SMS or email alert (or both, depending on contact info on file). The notification links directly into the portal, where they can log in and respond.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I send a message to multiple patients at once?">
  Yes — add multiple recipients in the **Start a new message** box. **However**, all recipients (including patients) see each other's names in the thread. For private bulk outreach, use **Outreach Flows** or **Text Blast** instead.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I attach files in a message?">
  Yes. Click **+ Add An Attachment** at the bottom of the message pane. Attachments are supported for both internal staff threads and external patient threads.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I find the Messages page in Insights?">
  Click **Messages** under **Utilities** in the sidebar. The **DM** tab shows all existing chats. The Notifications Center at the bottom of the sidebar also shows a count of unread messages and links directly to the thread.
</Accordion>
