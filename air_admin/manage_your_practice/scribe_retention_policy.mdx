The **Scribe Retention Policy** gives your practice control over how long [**AI Scribe**](/air_provider/fill_a_chart_note/scribe_faq) data is stored before it is automatically and permanently deleted. Configure a retention window to align AI Scribe data storage with your organization's privacy, security, and compliance requirements.

## How Scribe Retention Works

- **Site-level configuration.** Retention policies are set per site from your administrative settings.
- **What gets deleted.** Once a policy is active, AI Scribe **audio recordings**, **transcriptions**, and other associated context older than the configured window are removed.
- **Automatic, nightly cleanup.** Data that has aged past the retention window is deleted during the next nightly cleanup—no manual action is required.
- **Minimum window of 1 day.** The shortest retention window you can set is **1 day**.
- **Deletion is permanent.** Deleted AI Scribe data **cannot be recovered**.
- **Default is unchanged.** Sites without a configured retention policy continue to retain data as they do today—no deletion occurs.

<Warning>
  Scribe data deletion is **permanent and cannot be undone**. Confirm your retention window meets your clinical, legal, and compliance needs before saving.
</Warning>

## Configure a Retention Policy

To open the setting, navigate to **EHR Preferences → Administrative → Scribe**.

![Data retention settings under EHR Preferences, Administrative, Scribe](/images/air_admin/manage_your_practice/scribe_retention_policy/scribe_retention_policy_1.webp)

Under **Data retention**, the **Retention window (in days)** field controls how long scribe data is kept. When the field is empty, it reads **Not configured — no deletion will occur**, and your site retains all scribe data.

### Set a Retention Window

1. In the **Retention window (in days)** field, enter the number of days to keep AI Scribe data (minimum **1**).
2. Review any on-screen guidance:
   - A very short window shows a warning that it will delete nearly all scribe history.
   - A standard window shows no warning.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

**Very short window (warning):**

![Warning shown when a very short retention window is entered](/images/air_admin/manage_your_practice/scribe_retention_policy/scribe_retention_policy_2.webp)

**Standard window (no warning):**

![Standard retention window entered with no warning](/images/air_admin/manage_your_practice/scribe_retention_policy/scribe_retention_policy_3.webp)

### Confirm the Change

When you save, a confirmation dialog summarizes exactly what will be removed: all scribe data (audio, transcriptions, and additional context) older than your configured window will be permanently deleted during the next nightly cleanup.

Click **Confirm** to activate the policy, or **Cancel** to keep your current settings.

![Confirmation dialog before permanently deleting older scribe data](/images/air_admin/manage_your_practice/scribe_retention_policy/scribe_retention_policy_4.webp)

<Tip>
  ✨**Smart Tip:** Start with a longer retention window that comfortably covers your documentation and audit needs, then shorten it once you have confirmed the behavior on your site.
</Tip>

## Clear a Retention Policy

To stop automatic deletion, open **Data retention** and click **Clear**. The retention window returns to **Not configured — no deletion will occur**, and your site resumes retaining all scribe data going forward.

**Note:** Clearing the policy stops future deletions but does not restore data that was already deleted under a previous policy.

### FAQ

<Accordion title="What scribe data does the retention policy delete?">
  When a retention window is active, all AI Scribe data older than the configured number of days is removed. This includes **audio recordings**, **transcriptions**, and other associated context generated by AI Scribe.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When does deletion actually happen?">
  Data that has aged past your retention window is deleted during the next **nightly cleanup**. You do not need to trigger deletion manually.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the shortest retention window I can set?">
  The minimum retention window is **1 day**. If you enter a very short window, the platform warns you that it will delete nearly all scribe history and asks you to confirm before the change takes effect.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I recover scribe data after it is deleted?">
  No. Deletion is **permanent and cannot be undone**. Once data is removed during the nightly cleanup, it cannot be recovered.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I never configure a retention policy?">
  Nothing changes. Sites without a configured retention policy continue to retain AI Scribe data exactly as they do today—no automatic deletion occurs.
</Accordion>
