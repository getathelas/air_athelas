The **Settings** page is your central hub in Insights for your own account and for your practice's staff, facilities, providers, settings, and permissions.

<Note>
  **My Account** and **My Practice** are no longer separate pages. Both are now tabs on the **Settings** page, and old links to either page open the matching tab.
</Note>

## Open Settings

Click the gear icon next to your avatar at the bottom of the left navigation. If your left navigation ends with **Sign Out** instead, click **Settings** just above it.

To go straight to your own profile, open your avatar menu and choose **Account Settings**. It opens the **My Account** tab.

## Settings tabs

The tabs appear in this order:

| **Tab** | **What you manage there** |
| :-- | :-- |
| **My Account** | Your own profile. |
| **Team Members** | Users, roles, and facility access. See [User Management](/air_admin/manage_your_practice/user_management). |
| **Security** | Multi-factor authentication and the inactivity logout timeout for your practice. |
| **Practice Details** | Practice and corporate information. |
| **Main Contact** | Contact details for the practice owner and billing liaison. |
| **PR Settings** | Patient responsibility collection settings. See [PR Settings](/insights_front_desk/patient_responsibility/pr_settings). |
| **Logins** | Website and payer portal logins. |
| **Bank Accounts** | Your practice's bank accounts. |
| **Facilities** | Facilities, facility groups, and facility tags. |
| **Providers** | Provider records. See [Provider Records](/insights_admin/my_practice/provider_records). |
| **Taxonomy** | Provider taxonomy codes. |
| **Payers** | The payers your practice bills. |
| **Marketing** | Logos and display names for patient-facing surfaces. See [Branding Engine](/air_admin/manage_your_practice/branding_engine). |
| **Treatments** | Default treatments and their service codes. |

### Who sees which tabs

Every user sees **My Account**. The tabs after **My Account** need practice access. Administrators and Billing Managers have practice access by default, and an admin can grant other users practice access with the **My Practice** page permission. **Team Members**, **Security**, and **Bank Accounts** are narrower still: by default, only Administrators see those three tabs.

### **Team Members**

<Card>
  View, add, edit, and remove users. **Default Role Permissions:**

  - **Administrator** – Full access to all pages and features.
  - **Billing Manager** – Access to all pages except team management.
  - **Staff** – Limited access (Practice Overview \+ Advanced Insights). Best for front desk staff.
  - **Single Provider** – Claim Details page only, filtered to their own claims.

  ![Team Setting tab showing the user list, role filter, and Add new user, Edit, and Delete actions.](/images/air_admin/manage_your_practice/practice_settings/practice_settings_2.png)

  You may also check or uncheck individual items for specific employees.

  - The **Feature Permissions** options allow you to configure which features are available for your team member to utilize.
  - The **Page Permissions** options allow you to configure which pages your team member can view.

  <img
    src="/images/air_admin/manage_your_practice/practice_settings/practice_settings_3.png"
    alt="Role selection with Feature Permissions and Page Permissions checkboxes."
    title="Feature and Page Permissions"
    style={{ width:"73%" }}
  />

  **Note:** Unchecking “Miscellaneous Line Items” means a user cannot create or edit them, but can still view/apply them to balances.
</Card>

### **Practice Details**

<Card>
  Enter and update your practice and corporate information.

  Ensure the **corporate address** is your official billing address. This appears in Box 33 on the CMS-1500 form and is required for compliance and enrollments.
</Card>

### **Main Contact**

<Card>
  Store contact information for the **practice owner** and **billing liaison**.
</Card>

### **Facilities**

<Card>
  Add, edit, or deactivate your organization’s facilities.

  ![Facilities tab showing facilities grouped by region with Name, Phone, Billing Name, Tags, and NPI columns.](/images/air_admin/manage_your_practice/practice_settings/practice_settings_facilities.png)

  You can also move facilities between the **Active** and **Inactive** tabs.
</Card>

### **Providers**

<Card>
  Manage your practice’s healthcare providers.

  - Add, edit, or deactivate providers in the **Active tab**.
  - View deactivated providers in the **Inactive tab**, where you can reactivate or permanently delete them.

  ![Providers tab showing the active provider list with First Name, DOB, NPI, and License columns.](/images/air_admin/manage_your_practice/practice_settings/practice_settings_5.png)
</Card>
